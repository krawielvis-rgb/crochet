What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Sara Rain Crochet ("we", "us", or "the site") handles information when you visit crochet.krawielvis.workers.dev.

Information we collect

This site is primarily a public informational and tutorial website. We do not require an account to read our tutorials and we do not intentionally collect your name, address, phone number, or payment information through the site.

Like most websites, our hosting, security, advertising, and other technical services may process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, approximate location, and pages requested. This information may be used for security, performance, analytics, advertising measurement, and service operation.

Cookies & similar technologies

Third-party advertising and related technologies may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar identifiers. These technologies can be used to deliver, measure, limit, and personalize advertising, depending on the visitor's choices and applicable law. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

Advertising

We may use third-party advertising partners. Those partners may use their own cookies or similar technologies subject to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

If Google advertising services are enabled on this site, Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to serve and measure ads based on visits to this site and other sites. You can manage Google's personalized advertising choices through Google Ads Settings.

European, UK & Swiss visitors

Where required by applicable privacy and consent laws, advertising technologies will be used only according to the consent choices available to visitors. If Google advertising is enabled, the site will use an appropriate Google-supported or Google-certified consent management solution for applicable traffic. Google states that personalized ads for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland require a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

Links to other websites

Our tutorials and pages may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of websites that we do not operate. Please review their privacy policies before providing information.

Children

This site is general-audience crochet content and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where presented, through advertising consent controls. Blocking some cookies may affect advertising or parts of the site's functionality.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, advertising partners, or legal obligations change. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or correction requests, email sara.business.rain@gmail.com or use our Contact page.
