Cookie Policy

A clear explanation of cookies and similar technologies used on Sara Rain Crochet.

Last updated: September 14, 2026.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar identifiers stored on a device by a website or by a third-party service. Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, and other identifiers used for functionality, security, measurement, or advertising.

Essential technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for the site to operate, protect against abuse, maintain security, or provide requested functionality. These are different from optional advertising or personalization technologies where consent is required by applicable law.

Analytics and advertising

If analytics or advertising services are enabled, third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits, prevent fraud, understand advertising performance, and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

Google AdSense is not represented here as currently approved or active. If Google advertising is enabled in the future, the site's privacy and consent experience will be updated to reflect the applicable Google requirements.

Consent and your choices

Where applicable law requires consent, optional advertising and personalization technologies will be presented through an appropriate consent experience. Visitors can also control or delete cookies through their browser settings. Blocking some technologies can affect functionality or advertising.

European, UK and Swiss visitors

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, advertising-related consent will be handled in accordance with Google's publisher consent requirements and applicable law when Google advertising is enabled. Google requires a certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework for personalized ads in these regions.

Third-party policies

Third-party services have their own privacy and cookie policies. You should review those policies when optional advertising or analytics services are active.
