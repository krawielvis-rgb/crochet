Before you begin

I've been seeing ghost bag charms everywhere for Halloween 2026 — on tote bags, backpacks, even car mirrors — and I knew I had to make my own version. I wanted it spooky cute but still kawaii, not scary.

What I love about this pattern is it's mostly one piece. The ghost is crocheted top-down in one go — no sewing the body closed — we just add a little ruffled bottom edge for that wavy ghost look. The pumpkin and maple leaves are tiny 10-minute extras that make it look like the lifestyle pin you saw — with the tote bag and green backpack on the bench surrounded by fall leaves.

If you can do magic ring and single crochet, you can make this. I count out loud as I go so I don't get lost, and I use stitch markers for the increases.

Tip from my table: My ghost is about 2.5 inches tall (not counting the hanging chain). I use worsted cotton — it holds shape better than acrylic for charms and doesn't get fuzzy in your bag. Stuff lightly — a little goes a long way or it gets too puffy.

Materials I used

  • White yarn: Worsted cotton — I used Lily Sugar'n Cream white, about 25 yards for ghost. Cotton or acrylic both work, but cotton is cuter for bag charms.
  • Orange yarn: Small amount — about 5 yards each for pumpkin (orange) and maple leaves / star (rust orange / burnt orange). I used rust and burnt orange like my fall beanie.
  • Brown yarn: Tiny amount for leaf stems — about 1 yard.
  • 3.5mm crochet hook: Keeps stitches tight so stuffing doesn't show.
  • Safety eyes: 6mm black (2 pieces) — I add them between rounds 6-7, about 6 stitches apart.
  • Key ring + lobster clasp: Gold like in the pin, plus 2 jump rings.
  • BOO beads or wooden beads: Optional — I used wooden letter beads BOO in the first version.
  • Stuffing: Polyfill — just a pinch.
  • Tapeystry needle, scissors, stitch marker.

Abbreviations

MR = magic ring, sc = single crochet, inc = increase (2 sc in same st), dec = invisible decrease, sl st = slip stitch, ch = chain, FO = fasten off. We work in continuous rounds unless noted.

Full roadmap

Ghost Roadmap:
1. Ghost body (18 rounds, top-down)
2. Eyes + smile embroidery
3. Mini pumpkin (2 mins)
4. Maple leaves x2 + star
5. Hanging chains (brown, 15-18 ch each)
6. Assemble to key ring — clip to tote / backpack

Step 1: Crochet the ghost body

1Start top — magic ring with white

I start with white — this is the top of the head. I keep my tail long for hanging loop later.

Ghost Body - White:
R1: MR, 6 sc (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc, inc] x6 (18)
R4: [2 sc, inc] x6 (24)
R5-7: sc around (24) — 3 rounds straight
R8: [3 sc, inc] x6 (30)
R9-13: sc around (30) — 5 rounds, this is the chubby body
R14: [3 sc, dec] x6 (24) — start closing slightly
R15: sc around (24)
R16: [2 sc, dec] x6 (18) — stuff lightly here, I use tip of hook to push
R17: [sc, dec] x6 (12)
R18: FLO, [sc, inc, sc with picot] repeat around for ruffled bottom — I do (sc, ch 1, sl st in sc) for tiny wave
FO, weave in. Leave top loop.
How I do my ruffle: In last round I work in front loops only — I do sc, then ch 2 and sl st back into same stitch — gives that wavy ghost hem like in your pin. If you want simpler, just do [sc, 2 sc in next] around in FLO.

Step 2: Add eyes & face

2Face — my favorite part

I add safety eyes between R6 and R7 — about 5-6 stitches apart. If you don't have safety eyes, I embroider with black yarn — two little French knots. For smile — I do a tiny V with black yarn, and two pink blush lines with pink yarn on sides — just one stitch horizontal like in the lifestyle photo.

Tip: I put eyes before I close too much — easier to secure washers inside. Push stuffing to sides.

Step 3: Mini pumpkin — 5 minute make

3Tiny pumpkin — I made two, one orange with green stem
Pumpkin - Orange:
R1: MR, 6 sc (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc, inc] x6 (18)
R4-5: sc around (18)
R6: [sc, dec] x6 (12) — stuff tiny pinch
R7: dec x6 (6), FO close.
Stem: With green, ch 2, 2 sc in 2nd ch from hook, sl st to top of pumpkin. Or embroider 2 green stitches.

My pumpkin in the pin is about 0.8 inch — I make it with orange worsted and a little green stem. You can make one pumpkin for tote, one for backpack like I did.

Step 4: Maple leaves & star

4Maple leaves x2 + star — what makes it viral
Maple Leaf - Rust Orange (make 2):
Ch 4, sc in 2nd ch from hook, hdc in next, (hdc, ch 1, hdc) in last ch for tip, working other side: hdc, sc.
Add small points: ch 2, sl st to side of leaf, repeat on other side. FO leave tail for sewing.
Simpler version: I sometimes just do: MR, [ch 3, dc, ch 3, sl st] x3 — makes 3-point leaf in 1 round. Star (brown/rust):
MR, [ch 2, dc, ch 2, sl st] x5 — 5-point star. FO.

In your lifestyle pin you saw — my tote had one pumpkin + one orange leaf + one brown star. My backpack had one pumpkin + two orange maple leaves. I mix and match — that's the viral look.

Step 5: The hanging chains

5Brown hanging chains — connects everything to key ring

This is what makes it a bag charm, not just a plush.

Hanging Chains - Brown / Beige:
With brown yarn, ch 18 for left side, FO leave long tail.
Ch 15 for right side, FO.
Ch 10 for middle bottom (for leaf). FO.
I use 3 strands — left chain holds pumpkin, middle holds leaf, right holds star/leaf. In pin, left is pumpkin, middle leaf, right star.

Step 6: Assemble — like in your pin

6Assembly — clip to bag

1. Thread ghost top loop through key ring.
2. Thread two brown chains through same key ring, on either side of ghost — so ghost hangs in middle.
3. Sew pumpkin to end of left chain, star to right chain, leaf to middle bottom chain (or as you like). I use tapestry needle, sew through chain end.
4. Add lobster clasp to key ring — now you can clip to tote bag handle or backpack zipper like in the photo with the girl on the bench with fall leaves.
5. Optional: add BOO wooden beads to a separate small chain — I thread 3 beads B-O-O with small wooden spacer beads.

How I wear mine: I clipped one to my beige tote for farmer's market and one to my green backpack for fall walks — just like your pin with the bench, pumpkins, and orange leaves. They swing when you walk — so cute.

How I finish mine

I weave all ends inside ghost or pumpkin — I go back and forth 2 times so it doesn't come loose in bag. I spray a tiny bit of fabric stiffener on maple leaves so they keep shape — optional. I test the key ring — give it a tug — because bags get tossed.

Ways to make it your own

  • With pink bow: Like your first viral pin — add small pink bow on ghost head — ch 10, tie bow, sew.
  • With BOO beads: Add wooden letter beads B-O-O on separate chain — super viral for Halloween 2026.
  • Different colors: Make black ghost with orange bow, or orange ghost with black bow — 3 color variations like I made for your other pin.
  • Backpack version: Make chains longer (ch 22) so ghost hangs lower on backpack — like green backpack in photo.
  • Gift set: Make 2 — one for tote, one for backpack — sell as set — my bestseller at market.
