Sizing & Gauge

Gauge: Tight fabric key for keychains - if stuffing shows through black, go down to 3mm hook. With 3.5mm + worsted, 6 sc = 1 inch. Black yarn shows gaps more than light colors.

Finished size: Each bat body ~2" tall, ~1.25" wide, wings ~1.5" each, wingspan ~3" total. With beads + keyring ~4.5" total length. Fits in palm like Pin bottom close-up.

Keychain size checkpoint: 2" body is perfect - smaller than 1.5" wings look too big, larger than 2.5" heavy for keys. 3" wingspan shows orange wings detail well.

Abbreviations (US terms)

MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, inc = 2 sc in same st, dec = invisible decrease, st(s) = stitch(es), sl st = slip stitch, FO = fasten off, hdc = half double crochet, dc = double crochet

Step-by-Step Premium Pattern - Excellent Like Paid Pattern

1Magic Ring Black

Magic ring black - start bat body with black yarn, MR 6 sc, close no hole. Foundation for head+body one piece.

Round 1: With black yarn, MR, 6 sc into ring. Pull tail to close tightly. Place marker - essential for black yarn. [6 sts]
Round 2: inc in each st around. [12 sts]
Round 3: *sc 1, inc* around. [18 sts]
Round 4: *sc 2, inc* around. [24 sts] - Head width reached.

2Build Bat Body

Build bat body - black body head+body one piece, teardrop shape pointed at bottom like bat in Pin.

Round 5-7: sc in each st around. [24 sts] - 3 rounds straight for head.
Round 8: *sc 2, dec* around. [18 sts]
Round 9-10: sc around. [18 sts] - 2 rounds for neck transition.
Round 11: *sc 1, dec* around. [12 sts] - Body starts narrowing.
Round 12-13: sc around. [12 sts]
Round 14: *sc 2, dec* around. [9 sts]
Round 15: *sc 1, dec* around. [6 sts] Stuff lightly, FO, close with yarn needle. Teardrop bat body 2" tall pointed bottom like Pin.

3Black Ears

Black ears - 2 pointy ears per bat, small triangles on top of head like Pin.

Ear (make 2 per bat, 12 total for 6 bats) with black yarn:
Row 1: Ch 4, sc in 2nd ch, sc 2 across. Ch 1, turn. [3 sts]
Row 2: sc 3, ch 1, turn. [3 sts]
Row 3: dec, sc 1, ch 1, turn. [2 sts]
Row 4: dec. FO, leave 6" tail. [1 st] Pointy ear 0.5" tall like Pin.

4Orange Wings

Orange wings - 2 orange wings per bat, signature bat wing shape with points, orange yarn like Pin.

Wing (make 2 per bat, 12 total for 6 bats) with orange yarn + 3.5mm hook:
Row 1: Ch 6, sc in 2nd ch, sc 4 across. Ch 1, turn. [5 sts]
Row 2: sc 5, ch 1, turn. [5 sts]
Row 3: sc 1, hdc 1, dc 1, hdc 1, sc 1, ch 1, turn. [5 sts] - Creates wing point shape.
Row 4: dec, sc 1, dec. FO, leave 8" tail. [3 sts] Bat wing shape 1.5" wide with points like Pin orange wings.
Wing edge: For extra pointy bat wing, work 3 picots along bottom edge: *sc, ch 3, sl st in first ch* repeat 3 times.

5Attach Wings

Attach wings - sew orange wings to sides of black body, placement key for bat look.

Attach: Pin orange wings to sides of black body, just below head, angled slightly upward like bat flying. With tail and yarn needle, whip stitch around base of each wing through body. Go around base twice for security - wings get pulled on keychains, need strong join.
Position: Wings should be symmetrical, same height, angled upward like Pin. Bottom edge of wing at middle of body.

6Attach Ears

Attach ears - sew black ears to top of head, small and pointed like Pin.

Attach: Pin black ears to top of head, about 5 sts apart, slightly angled outward. With tail and yarn needle, whip stitch around base of each ear through head. Go around base twice for security - keychains get pulled a lot.
Weave ends: Pull tails inside head to hide, knot inside for strength.

7Eyes + Pink Cheeks

Eyes + pink cheeks - big eyes + pink blush cheeks give bat cute personality like Pin, not scary.

Green/Black safety eyes: Insert 8mm safety eyes between Rounds 4-5, 6 sts apart, centered. Push washers inside until click. Test secure - pull hard, keychains get yanked.
Pink cheeks embroidery: With pink yarn and yarn needle, embroider small circles or horizontal stitches just below and slightly outside each eye. 2-3 wraps for plump cheeks like Pin. Cheeks about 1 st below eyes, 1 st outside, makes bat blushing cute.
Smile (optional): Small black smile between eyes, 2 sts wide, backstitch.

Child-safe alternative: Embroider eyes with black yarn satin stitch instead of safety eyes.

8Keychain Assembly - Wooden Beads + Gold Keyring!

Keychain assembly - final step that makes it a keychain with wooden beads and gold keyring like Pin!

Assembly per bat:
1. Thread yarn needle with strong thread or thin yarn through top of head between ears.
2. Pull through, leave loop at top.
3. Add beads in order: small gold jump ring → 3 wooden beads (natural color like Pin) → gold keyring.
4. Secure with knot inside head, weave tail in, trim, add drop of fabric glue on knot inside for keychain strength.
5. Attach lobster clasp to keyring if using as bag charm.

Troubleshooting - Black Yarn, Wings, Ears

Can't see stitches in black yarn

Most common issue! Use bright white LED light, white paper under work, stitch markers every 6 sts, count out loud. Touch to feel stitches. Some crocheters use light-colored stitch markers clipped to each st for black yarn.

White stuffing shows through black body

Hook too big or over-stuffed. Go down to 3mm hook, use black fiberfill if available, or use less stuffing - body should be firm not rock hard, lightly stuffed for keychain lightweight.

Orange wings not pointy bat shape, look rounded

Follow Row 3-4 exactly: Row 3 creates point with hdc/dc/hdc, Row 4 decreases to 3 sts point. Add picots along bottom edge for extra bat points: *sc, ch 3, sl st in first ch* repeat 3 times.

Ears or wings fall off keychain

Keychains get pulled hard - sew extra secure: go through body fabric deep, not just surface, whip stitch twice around base, knot inside and glue knot with fabric glue.

Wings not symmetrical

Pin both wings before sewing, measure from top of head, same round. Use stitch markers to mark wing placement left/right, ensure same height.

Safety eyes won't click in black yarn

Black yarn thick - push harder with flat surface behind head, or move eyes 1 round higher where fabric less dense. Test pull hard - if washer pops off, use embroidered eyes instead.

Care & Storage

  • Spot clean only - black yarn shows lint, use lint roller gently.
  • Keychains get dirty from hands - hand wash cold gentle occasionally, squeeze in towel, air dry, reshape wings while damp.
  • Check keyring hardware monthly - jump rings can loosen with use, tighten with pliers.
  • Store hanging or in pouch - keep beads from scratching and wings from bending.
  • Keep away from pets - small parts (beads) choking hazard, plus cats may attack bat keychains!
