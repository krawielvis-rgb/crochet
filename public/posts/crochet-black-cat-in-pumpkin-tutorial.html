Before You Begin

This cute black cat in orange crochet pumpkin is the most viral Halloween amigurumi for 2025 - black cat with green eyes, pink cheeks, pointy ears, and green leaves decoration sitting in a crochet pumpkin. It's beginner-friendly because body is just a round ball, ears are small triangles, and pumpkin is simple orange ball with ridges.

The sample in your Pin uses black yarn + 3.5mm hook, green safety eyes, pink yarn for cheeks, green yarn for leaves, orange yarn for pumpkin. Black yarn can be hard to see, so we use stitch markers every round and bright light - explained in troubleshooting.

Why this is beginner-proof: Round head is just increase then straight then decrease - no complex shaping. Ears are 2 small triangles sewn on. Green eyes and pink cheeks are added after head is crocheted, so you can position them perfectly. Green leaves hide any uneven top stitches.

Materials

From your Pin:

  • Black yarn (worsted): 40 yards for cat head and ears. Soft cotton or acrylic that doesn't split.
  • Orange yarn (worsted): 30 yards for crochet pumpkin.
  • Green yarn: Small amount for leaves, plus green safety eyes or embroidered eyes.
  • Pink yarn: Small amount for cheeks embroidery.
  • 3.5mm crochet hook: Creates tight fabric for amigurumi so stuffing doesn't show.
  • 12mm green safety eyes: Pair, or embroider eyes with green yarn for child-safe version.
  • Fiberfill, yarn needle, scissors, stitch markers: Essential for black yarn.

Sizing & Gauge

Gauge: Not critical for decor, but tight fabric is key for black yarn - if you see white stuffing through black stitches, go down to 3mm hook. With 3.5mm + worsted, 6 sc = 1 inch.

Finished size: Black cat head ~2.5" diameter, ears ~1" tall, pumpkin ~3.5" wide. Cat sits inside pumpkin like Pin.

Black yarn checkpoint: Use bright white light behind work and stitch markers every 6 sts. Count out loud. Black yarn hides stitches easily.

Abbreviations (US terms)

MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, inc = 2 sc in same st, dec = invisible decrease, st(s) = stitch(es), sl st = slip stitch, FO = fasten off

3. Round head: Join circles into round head ball
4. Eyes pink cheeks: Green eyes, pink cheeks embroidery

Step-by-Step Premium Pattern - Excellent Like Paid Pattern

1Magic Ring Black

Magic ring black - start with black yarn, MR 6 sc, close with no hole. This is foundation for cat head.

Round 1: With black yarn, MR, 6 sc into ring. Pull tail to close tightly. Place marker. [6 sts]
Round 2: inc in each st around. [12 sts]
Round 3: *sc 1, inc* repeat around. [18 sts]
Tip for black yarn: Place white paper under work, use bright light, stitch marker in first st every round.

2Two Black Circles

Two black circles - make two flat black circles 24 sts each, will become base for round head ball.

Circle (make 2):
Round 1: MR, 6 sc. [6 sts]
Round 2: inc around. [12 sts]
Round 3: *sc 1, inc* around. [18 sts]
Round 4: *sc 2, inc* around. [24 sts] FO first circle, leave tail. Make second circle but do not FO - will join to first to form ball in next step.
Checkpoint: Both circles flat, same size 24 sts, no curling.

3Round Head

Round head - join circles into round head ball, simple sphere that becomes cat head.

Join into ball:
Hold two circles together. With second circle still on hook, sc through both circles around edge to join: sc 24 around. Now you have 24 sts forming top edge.
Round 5-9: sc in each st around. [24 sts] - 5 rounds straight creates 2" tall round head ball.
Round 10: *sc 2, dec* repeat around. [18 sts]
Round 11: *sc 1, dec* repeat around. [12 sts] Stuff with fiberfill now.
Round 12: dec around. [6 sts] FO, close with yarn needle.

4Eyes Pink Cheeks

Eyes pink cheeks - green eyes and pink cheeks give black cat cute personality, like Pin.

Green eyes: Insert 12mm green safety eyes between Rounds 6-7, 6 sts apart, centered. Push washers inside until click. Test secure.
Pink cheeks embroidery: With pink yarn and yarn needle, embroider small circles or horizontal stitches just below and slightly outside each eye. 2-3 wraps for plump cheeks.
Position: Cheeks about 1 st below eyes, 1 st outside. Makes cat look blushing cute.

Child-safe alternative: Embroider green eyes with satin stitch instead of safety eyes.

5Ears

Ears - crochet 2 black pointy ears, small triangles that define cat silhouette.

Ear (make 2 with black yarn):
Row 1: Ch 5, sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc 3 across. Ch 1, turn. [4 sts]
Row 2: sc 4, ch 1, turn. [4 sts]
Row 3: dec, dec, ch 1, turn. [2 sts]
Row 4: dec. FO, leave 8" tail for sewing. [1 st]
Shape: Triangle pointy ear, about 1" tall. Make second identical.

6Attach Ears

Attach ears - sew ears to top of head, placement is key for cute cat look.

Attach: Pin ears to top of head, about 6 sts apart, slightly angled outward (cuter than straight up). With tail and yarn needle, whip stitch around base of each ear through head. Go around base twice for security.
Weave ends: Pull tails inside head to hide.

7Green Leaves

Green leaves - crochet green leaves decoration, adds Halloween autumn touch like Pin.

Leaf (make 2-3 with green yarn + 3.5mm hook):
Row 1: Ch 7, sc in 2nd ch, hdc, dc, hdc, sc, sl st in last ch. Working other side of chain: sc, hdc, dc, hdc, sc, sl st to first st. FO, leave tail.
Sew: Sew leaves to sides of cat head or around base where cat meets pumpkin. In Pin, leaves are on sides of head and on pumpkin top.
Stem (optional): Small green stem on top of head: Ch 4, sc 3, FO, sew to top center between ears.

8Final Cute Black Cat in Orange Crochet Pumpkin

Final cute black cat in orange crochet pumpkin - your Halloween decor is ready!

Orange crochet pumpkin (quick pattern):
Round 1: With orange yarn, MR, 6 sc. [6]
Round 2: inc around. [12]
Round 3: *sc 1, inc* around. [18]
Round 4: *sc 2, inc* around. [24]
Round 5: *sc 3, inc* around. [30]
Round 6-11: sc around. [30] - 6 rounds straight.
Round 12: *sc 3, dec* around. [24]
Round 13: *sc 2, dec* around. [18] Stuff lightly.
Round 14: *sc 1, dec* around. [12]
Round 15: dec around. [6] FO, close, add ridges by sewing through center with orange yarn 6 times.
Display: Place black cat inside or on top of pumpkin. Cat paws over edge like Pin for cute peek.

Progress Checkpoints

  • Checkpoint 1 - Magic ring black: MR closed no hole, 6 sts, marker in first st.
  • Checkpoint 2 - Two black circles: 2 flat circles 24 sts each, same size, flat not curling.
  • Checkpoint 3 - Round head: Sphere 24 sts, 5 rounds straight, stuffed firm, no stuffing showing through black.
  • Checkpoint 4 - Eyes pink cheeks: Green eyes 6 sts apart between Rounds 6-7, pink cheeks 1 st below eyes, blushing cute.
  • Checkpoint 5 - Ears: 2 pointy black ears 1" tall, triangle shape, tails 8".
  • Checkpoint 6 - Attach ears: Ears 6 sts apart on top, angled outward, sewn twice around base secure.
  • Checkpoint 7 - Green leaves: 2-3 green leaves sewn to sides/top, small stem optional between ears.
  • Checkpoint 8 - Final: Black cat sits in orange pumpkin, paws over edge like Pin, cute photo ready.

Troubleshooting - Black Yarn Tips

Can't see stitches in black yarn

Most common issue! Use bright white LED light, white paper under work, stitch markers every 6 sts, count out loud. Touch to feel stitches. Some crocheters use light-colored stitch markers clipped to each st.

White stuffing shows through black fabric

Hook too big or over-stuffed. Go down to 3mm hook, use black fiberfill if available, or use less stuffing - head should be firm not rock hard.

Ears not pointy, look rounded

Decreased too slow. Follow Row 1-4 exactly: 4→4→2→1 creates point. If still rounded, use smaller hook for ears only (3mm).

Green eyes won't click

Push harder with flat surface behind head. Black yarn thick - may need to move eyes 1 round higher where fabric less dense.

Cat head not round, looks oval

Stuffing uneven. Add small amounts at a time, push to sides, then center. Head should be sphere.

Pumpkin ridges not defined

After closing pumpkin, use orange yarn to sew through center from top to bottom 6 times, pulling tight each time to create ridges like real pumpkin.

Finishing & Display in Orange Crochet Pumpkin

Weave all black tails inside head: 4 sts forward, 3 back opposite direction, trim close. White stuffing shows less if woven in dark area.

Display ideas: Orange crochet pumpkin with black cat peeking out over edge (paws over edge like Pin), real mini orange pumpkin with cat sitting inside, tiered tray with autumn leaves, fairy lights, small hay, cinnamon sticks. Perfect Halloween tiered tray decor.

Photo tip: Shoot with cat paws over pumpkin edge, slightly above angle, bokeh fairy lights background - viral Pinterest style like Pin. Add text overlay "Cute and Easy 8-Step Tutorial - Crochet Black Cat in Pumpkin".

Care & Storage

  • Spot clean only - black yarn shows lint. Use lint roller gently.
  • If needed, hand wash cold gentle, squeeze in towel (don't wring), lay flat to dry, reshape head and ears while damp.
  • Store in pumpkin or display year-round - black cat is cute beyond Halloween (cat lovers).
  • Check safety eyes monthly if child's toy - ensure washers secure.
  • Keep away from pets - small parts (eyes) could be chewed, plus cats may be confused by cat toy!
