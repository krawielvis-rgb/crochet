How an AirPods case works — two parts, 36 to 36 hinge

An AirPods crochet cover has two parts: bottom holds the AirPods box, top is the axolotl head that opens. They join at back with 5 stitches like a door hinge.

Both have same oval opening — 36 stitches — so hinge lines up. Bottom is bowl open 36 at top, top is hat closed dome at top and open 36 at bottom. When closed, top sits over bottom like axolotl mouth closed — like in your photo with AirPods inside open mouth.

Width reconciled: same 36-stitch oval is 3" wide when laid flat and unstretched on table, but 2.4" wide × 1.8" deep when stretched around AirPods case 2.3"×1.7". Same piece, two measurements — one flat, one stretched.

Bottom — oval bowl — 24→30→36 — precise math

BOTTOM — BOWL — OPEN 36
Bottom — pink, 3.5mm — oval bowl — open at top 36:
R1: ch 12, sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc 9, 3 sc in last ch, turn to other side of chain, sc 9, 2 sc in last — 24 sc — oval — place marker at start
R2: inc, sc 9, inc 3 times, sc 9, inc 2 times — 30 sc — 6 inc total — 24+6=30
R3: sc, inc, sc 9, [sc, inc] 3 times, sc 9, [sc, inc] 2 times — 36 sc — base is 3" wide flat unstretched, 2.4" wide ×1.2" deep stretched around case — check with AirPods case on top — should be slightly bigger
R4: BLO sc around — 36 sc — BLO = back loop only — makes sharp edge for walls to go straight up — important
R5-18: sc around — 36 sc each round — 14 rounds straight — walls go up — 1.8" tall from BLO edge — no inc, no dec — try AirPods case inside at R12 — should fit snug
Fasten off — leave long tail — bottom finished — 36 stitches at top edge — open bowl — 3" wide flat, 2.4"×1.8" stretched

36 at R3 is the number that fits AirPods case. 36 sc at 6 sc per inch = 6 inches around ÷2 sides = 3" wide flat. Stretched around case it's 2.4" wide ×1.8" deep — perfect for AirPods Gen 1/2/3 and Pro case 2.3"×1.7". I checked at R12 with my own AirPods case inside.

TOP — HEAD HAT — DECIDED: OPEN 36 BOTTOM, CLOSED DOME TOP

Gills — 6 fluffy — my brushing trick

GILLS — 6 FLUFFY — 0.8 INCH
Gills — make 6 — pink + fluffy pink, 3.5mm:
Each gill: ch 8, sc in 2nd ch, sc 6 — 7 sc — Row 1
Row 2-3: ch 1, sc 7 — 2 rows — flat gill 0.8" ×0.4"
Fluff: cut 6 strands fluffy pink 2" long, lark's head knot through tip stitches — 3 strands per side of tip — brush gently with cat slicker brush until fluffy — like in your photo
Make 6 — 3 per side of head — attach at R8-10 of top — spaced 0.4" apart — 3 left, 3 right — sew with pink tail — fluff outwards

Gills are what makes axolotl cute. Small flat flap + fluffy yarn brushed — genuinely clever trick — reads clean like my other posts.

Face — eyes + smile

FACE
Face — on top head:
Eyes: 8mm safety eyes at R12 of top, 6 stitches apart, with 0.5" white felt circles behind — poke eye through felt then through crochet — shiny look like photo
Eyelash: black scrap — 2 stitches at outer corner
Smile: pink scrap — small W — 3 stitches wide

Legs and tail

LEGS ×4 + TAIL
Legs — make 4 — pink, 3.5mm:
R1: magic ring 6 sc — 6
R2-3: sc 6 — 2 rounds — 0.6" tall
Sew to bottom sides — 2 front, 2 back — 0.3" from bottom edge

Tail — pink + fluffy:
R1: ch 6, sc 5 — 5
R2-5: ch 1, sc 5 — 4 rows — 1" long flat tail
Fluff tip: 3 fluffy strands at tip like gills — brush — sew to back center of bottom at R2-3

Assembly — hinge 5 sc — fit test

ASSEMBLY — HINGE 36 TO 36
Hinge — bottom 36 to top 36:
Place bottom and top with openings together, back sides aligned — sew 5 sc at back through both layers — back hinge — 5 stitches — front stays open like mouth — whip stitch
Try AirPods case inside bottom — should fit snug — 2.4"×1.8" stretched — top closes over like mouth — like in your photo
Weave tails — pink inside pink — 3 stitches forward, 3 back

Fit test: AirPods case inside bottom — if tight, stretch cotton with fingers — cotton stretches.

Finished
