Minimal Net Bag — Slow Made Mesh Bag 🤍🪵

A slow made cream mesh net bag with wooden ring handles — minimal, airy, sturdy — perfect for market, beach, slow living, everyday minimal carry.

I made this one on a quiet morning with soft light and linen on my table — I had cream cotton yarn and wooden rings, just like in your photo with cream net bag hanging on off-white wall with wooden ring handles and "slow made" and "MINIMAL NET BAG" text. A friend who loves minimal living asked for a bag that was airy and strong — "Can you make one with wooden rings?" So I sat with cream yarn, 5mm hook, and two wooden rings 5" diameter, and crocheted a net while tea steamed. By afternoon it was holding my market bread, and I hung it on the wall. It was so minimal everyone asked where I got it.

How this minimal net bag is made — base + mesh + wooden rings

This bag is made from bottom up — solid base circle about 4" wide (36 sts), then diamond mesh net about 15" tall with 18 holes around, each hole about 1" wide, then solid top edge for wooden ring handles — two wooden rings 5" diameter attached at top — bag about 14" wide ×15" tall — mesh holes about 1" — holds market veggies, beach towels, slow living — like in your photo with cream net bag hanging with wooden rings.

Mesh net — diamond openwork — 15" tall — 18 holes around — 1" holes

MESH NET — CREAM — 5MM — DIAMOND OPENWORK — 15 INCH TALL — 18 HOLES AROUND — 1 INCH HOLES
Mesh net — cream, 5mm hook — openwork mesh sides — continuing from base without cutting yarn:
R7: continuing from R6 — do not cut yarn — ch 4 (counts as dc + ch1), *skip 1 st of R6 edge, dc in next st of R6, ch1* repeat around — working directly into R6 edge — 18 dc + 18 ch1 spaces — 18 mesh holes around — 36 sts of base become 18 mesh repeats because you skip every other st — join with sl st to 3rd chain of ch4 — first mesh round — holes about 1" — mesh now connected to base
R8-24: ch 4, *dc in ch1 space from previous round, ch1* repeat around — offset by half a stitch each round — dc goes into ch1 space, not into dc below — this offset creates diamond-shaped holes rather than straight stacked columns — 18 dc + 18 ch1 spaces each round — 17 rounds of mesh — each round about 0.85" tall — 17 rounds ×0.85" =14.45" tall — plus base 0.5" =14.95" tall total — about 15" tall total — openwork diamond net with 1" diamond holes — 18 holes around — diamond pattern from offset — 14" wide when flat, stretches to 16" when full
Check after R15 — halfway — about 7.5" tall mesh — 18 diamond mesh holes around — 14" wide flat — fits market
After R24 — 15" tall total — 14.5" mesh +0.5" base =15" tall — 18 diamond mesh holes around — openwork diamond mesh like in your photo with cream net bag 1" diamond holes — mesh bag sides — diamond shape from offset each round — mesh finished
R25: sc 36 — final solid round at top edge — sc in each dc and ch1 space — 36 sc — solid top edge for wooden rings — top edge finished — 36 sc — 14" wide flat

Mesh net is 18 dc + 18 ch1 spaces — 18 mesh holes around — each hole about 1" — 17 rounds of mesh offset — each round about 0.85" tall — 17 rounds ×0.85" =14.45" tall plus base 0.5" =15" tall total — you work R7 directly into R6 base edge without cutting yarn, so 36 base sts become 18 mesh repeats by skipping every other st — then R8-24 you offset by working dc into ch1 space from previous round rather than into dc below, which creates diamond-shaped holes rather than straight stacked columns — openwork diamond mesh like in your photo with cream net bag with 1" diamond holes — 14" wide flat, stretches to 16" when full — mesh bag sides.
