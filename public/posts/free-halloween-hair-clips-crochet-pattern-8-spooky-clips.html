The Halloween Clip Collection — 8 Tiny Crochet Clips I Make Every October

Ghost, pumpkin, black cat, witch hat, bat, skull, candy corn, and spiderweb — all under 1.5 inches, all no-sew or one-sew, all stitched directly onto a 1.5 inch alligator clip.

Why I love tiny clips for Halloween

Big amigurumi is cozy but tiny clips are instant joy. You finish one while your kettle boils. Kids can wear two or three at once — ghost and bat together is my favorite combo — and they don't fall off like headbands.

Last year I made the ghost first. It's just a dome that doesn't close — 6 to 18, then straight down. My niece put it on and said it looked like it was flying. That was it, I had to make the whole set. I kept the math really small so beginners can do them without getting lost in counting.

I clip them onto a piece of cardboard shaped like a little tree at my market table. People always touch the candy corn one first.

What you need — my little Halloween basket

  • Yarn: DK cotton — White 20g, Orange 15g, Black 20g, Purple 10g, Yellow 10g, plus tiny pink for cat nose
  • Hook: 3.0mm — smaller hook keeps tiny pieces tight and no stuffing shows
  • Clips: 1.5" alligator clips (silver or black), 8 pieces — I get them in 20-packs
  • Extras: 6mm safety eyes or black embroidery thread, tiny bit polyfill for pumpkin/ghost/cat, tapestry needle, hot glue or needle to sew onto clip
For hair clips, I don't use safety eyes on pieces kids under 3 will wear — I embroider. For my own clips I use 6mm eyes. Place them between R4-R5, 4 stitches apart for ghost/cat/skull.

My pumpkin — 6→18 ridged, stem on top. This is the one I make first to warm up.

The base clip I use

I use 1.5 inch alligator clips with teeth. I crochet a small rectangle base — ch 6, sc 5 rows — glue or sew it inside the clip's top, then sew the tiny amigurumi onto that base. It holds way better than gluing yarn directly to metal. Takes 2 minutes extra and they never fall off.

Clip 1: Ghost — 6→18, no sewing

CLIP 1 · GHOST
Ghost — in white, 3.0mm:
R1: mr, 6 sc (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc 1, inc] x6 (18)
R4-R7: sc around 18 — 4 rounds even (18)
R8: [sc 2, inc] x6 (24) — makes wavy bottom — sc 24
FO, leave tail. No stuffing or light stuffing with a tiny puff.
Face: Embroider 2 black eyes between R4-R5, 4 sts apart, and tiny mouth.
Sew onto clip base.

My ghost is the fastest. The wavy edge at R8 is what gives it that floating look — don't skip it. I make 3 in a row assembly-line style.

Black cat — same base as ghost but with ears. Yellow eyes embroidered, pink nose.

Clip 2: Pumpkin — ridged ball with stem

CLIP 2 · PUMPKIN
Pumpkin — orange body + green stem:
R1: mr, 6 (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc 1, inc] x6 (18)
R4-R6: sc 18 — 3 rounds
R7: [sc 1, dec] x6 (12) — stuff lightly
R8: dec x6 (6) — close
Ridge: With orange yarn and needle, wrap 6 times around from top to bottom, pulling slightly — makes pumpkin segments
Stem: Green — mr 4, sc 4 — 2 rounds, FO, sew on top.

This is the one in the photo you sent — the ridges make it look like a real tiny pumpkin. I pull the wrap yarn quite tight, then knot inside. Add a tiny green leaf — ch 4, sc, hdc, sc — if you want.

Clip 3: Black Cat — yellow eyes like yours

CLIP 3 · BLACK CAT
Black cat — in black:
R1: mr, 6 (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc 1, inc] x6 (18)
R4-R6: sc 18 — 3 rounds (18)
R7: [sc 1, dec] x6 (12) — stuff
R8: dec x6 (6) — close
Ears x2: Black — mr 3, [sc 1, inc] x1 (4), sc 4 — FO, sew on top between R1-R2, 4 sts apart
Face: Yellow eyes embroidered — tiny circles — between R4-R5, 4 sts apart, pink nose, white whiskers

Black yarn is hard to see — I put a white towel on my lap and use a headlamp. Yellow eyes make it pop. This is the most popular one at markets after ghost.

Clip 4: Witch Hat — purple with brim

CLIP 4 · WITCH HAT
Witch hat — in purple:
R1: mr, 4 (4)
R2: [sc 1, inc] x2 (6)
R3: sc 6
R4: [sc 2, inc] x2 (8)
R5: sc 8
R6: [sc 3, inc] x2 (10)
Brim: FLO — [sc 1, inc] x5 (15), sc around, FO
Add yellow star: mr, [ch 1, dc, ch 1, sl st] x5 — tiny, sew on.

Same shape as my frog witch hat but mini. Brim is what makes it stay on clip — I sew through brim only. Leave tip floppy.

My full set — 8 tiny clips on cardboard. I make 2 sets at a time, assembly line.

Clip 5: Bat — tiny wings

CLIP 5 · BAT
Bat — body black, wings black:
Body: mr 6, inc x6 (12), sc 12 — 2 rounds, [sc 1, dec] x4 (8), stuff lightly, dec x4 (4) — close
Wings x2: Ch 6, sc in 2nd ch, hdc, dc, hdc, sc — wing shape — FO
Sew wings to sides of body, add tiny ears — mr 3 sc — 2 pieces
Eyes: white embroidery dots

Clip 6: Skull — white with black eyes

CLIP 6 · SKULL
Skull — white:
R1: mr, 6 (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc 1, inc] x6 (18)
R4-R5: sc 18 — 2 rounds
R6: [sc 1, dec] x6 (12) — stuff
R7: sc 12 — FO
Face: Embroider 2 black ovals for eyes between R3-R4, 3 sts apart, small black nose triangle, small line for teeth — 3 vertical lines

Clip 7: Candy Corn — 3-color triangle

CLIP 7 · CANDY CORN
Candy corn — white, orange, yellow — flat, no stuffing:
White R1: mr 3, sc 3, ch 1 turn
R2: inc, sc 1, inc (5)
Switch orange R3: sc 5
R4: inc, sc 3, inc (7)
Switch yellow R5: sc 7
R6: sc 7 — FO, border sc around whole triangle in yellow for clean edge

This one is flat, no stuffing — I love how the 3 colors stack. Make sure to carry yarn up the side neatly or cut and weave.

Clip 8: Spiderweb — web on black

CLIP 8 · SPIDERWEB
Spiderweb — black base + white web:
Base: Black — mr 6, inc x6 (12), [sc 1, inc] x6 (18) — flat circle
Web: With white, embroider — 6 spokes from center to edge, then 2 circles connecting spokes — like real web
Optional: tiny white spider — mr 4, sc 4, 4 legs — ch 3 each side

The web is embroidery, not crochet — faster and looks cleaner at 1 inch. I use white sewing thread doubled so it's fine.

How I attach to clips so they stay

1. Crochet base rectangle: ch 6, sc 5 rows. 2. Hot glue or sew base inside top of alligator clip. 3. Sew each tiny piece onto base with matching yarn, 3-4 stitches. Don't glue amigurumi directly — it pops off. Base method never fails for me.

If they curl or flop

Ghost wavy bottom curls too much: You increased to 24 in R8 — that's meant to wave. If it rolls, steam lightly.

Black cat hard to see stitches: White towel on lap, bright light, count by feel — 6,12,18.

Pumpkin not round: Stuff before closing R7-R8, not after.

Clips slide out of hair: Use clips with teeth and add small silicone strip inside or tease hair slightly.

Make them yours

I made a pastel set — pink ghost, lavender bat — for a little girl who doesn't like spooky. You can also make them as earrings — just add jump rings instead of clips. If you make the set, I would love to see which one is your favorite — mine is still ghost.
