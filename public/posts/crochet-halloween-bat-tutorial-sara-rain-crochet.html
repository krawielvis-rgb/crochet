Before You Begin

This cute bat is designed as a first Halloween amigurumi. The body is a simple oval - no complex shaping. Wings are flat triangles you sew on. Ears are tiny triangles. The whole bat fits in the palm of your hand and sits perfectly inside a mini pumpkin for display.

The sample in the Pin uses black yarn for body and orange yarn for wings, 4mm hook, 8mm safety eyes, and fiberfill stuffing. The orange wings make it pop for Halloween photos.

Why this is beginner-proof: Black yarn can be hard to see, so we use stitch markers in every round. Wings are crocheted separately and sewn on - no joining while crocheting body. Safety eyes go in before stuffing, so no fiddling at the end.

Materials

From your Pin:

  • Black & orange yarn (worsted / medium): 30 yards black, 15 yards orange. Soft acrylic or cotton blend. Black for body/ears, orange for wings.
  • 4mm crochet hook (G-6): Standard for amigurumi - creates tight fabric so stuffing doesn't show.
  • Stuffing / fiberfill: Small amount - about a handful.
  • 8mm safety eyes (black): Pair. With washers.
  • Yarn needle & scissors: For sewing wings/ears and weaving ends. Stitch markers optional but highly recommended for black yarn.

Also nice: Pink embroidery thread for cheeks, small orange pumpkin for display.

Sizing & Gauge

Gauge: Not critical for amigurumi, but tight fabric is key. With 4mm hook + worsted, 6 sc = 1 inch, 7 rounds = 1 inch. If your stitches have gaps where stuffing shows, go down to 3.5mm.

Finished size: Bat ~3" tall (without wings), ~4" wide with wings spread. Fits perfectly in 3-4" mini pumpkin.

Fit checkpoint: Fabric should be dense with no visible holes. If you can see stuffing through stitches, go down a hook size.

Abbreviations (US terms)

MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, inc = 2 sc in same st, dec = invisible decrease (sc2tog), st(s) = stitch(es), sl st = slip stitch, FO = fasten off

Complete Roadmap - 8 Steps

1. Magic Ring & Body: Start with black yarn, make magic ring, 6 sc
2. Build the Body: Work 6 rounds in sc to form oval body
3. Crochet Wings: Switch to orange yarn, make wing (make 2)
4. Attach Wings: Sew wings to each side of body
5. Make Ears: Crochet 2 small ears, sew to top
6. Add Eyes & Details: Add 8mm safety eyes, pink cheek embroidery
7. Stuff & Close: Stuff with fiberfill, close opening
8. Display in Pumpkin: Your bat is ready for Halloween!

Step-by-Step Premium Pattern - Excellent Like Paid Pattern

1Magic Ring & Body

Start with black yarn, make magic ring, 6 sc - this is the foundation that closes with no hole.

Round 1: MR, 6 sc into ring. Pull tail to close tightly. Place marker. [6 sts]
Round 2: inc in each st around. [12 sts]
Round 3: *sc 1, inc* repeat around. [18 sts]
Tip for black yarn: Count stitches after each round. Use stitch marker and count out loud - black yarn hides stitches.

2Build the Body

Work 6 rounds in sc to form body - simple oval that grows then stays straight.

Round 4: *sc 2, inc* repeat around. [24 sts]
Round 5-9: sc in each st around. [24 sts] - 5 rounds straight creates oval body length.
Round 10: *sc 2, dec* repeat around. [18 sts] - starts closing bottom.
Do not stuff yet. Leave opening for safety eyes insertion.

3Crochet Wings - Orange Yarn

Switch to orange yarn, make wing - you will make 2 identical wings. Wings are flat, worked in rows, triangular bat-wing shape.

Wing (make 2 with orange yarn):
Row 1: Ch 7, sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc 5 across. Ch 1, turn. [6 sts]
Row 2: inc, sc 4, inc, ch 1, turn. [8 sts]
Row 3: sc across, ch 1, turn. [8 sts]
Row 4: inc, sc 6, inc, ch 1, turn. [10 sts]
Row 5: sc 3, hdc 2, dc 2, hdc 2, sc 1 - creates pointed bottom. FO, leave 10" tail for sewing.

Second wing: Repeat exactly. You should have 2 mirror-image orange wings.

4Attach Wings

Sew wings to each side of body - symmetrical placement is key for cute look.

Attach: Position wings on sides of body, about Round 5-6 height. Use yarn needle with orange tail, whip stitch wing to body through 4-5 stitches per wing. Sew firmly - wings get handled.
Check: Wings should stick out sideways, not droop. If drooping, sew one extra stitch at top of wing.

5Make Ears - 2 Small Ears, Sew to Top

Crochet 2 small ears, sew to top - tiny triangles that give bat its character.

Ear (make 2 with black yarn):
Round 1: MR, 4 sc into ring. [4 sts]
Round 2: *sc 1, inc* twice. [6 sts]
Round 3: sc 6. FO, leave 8" tail.
Sew: Pin ears to top of head, about 4 sts apart. Sew around base of each ear with tail. Weave ends inside body.

6Add Eyes & Details - 8mm Safety Eyes, Pink Cheek Embroidery

Add 8mm safety eyes, pink cheek embroidery - this is where bat gets its cute personality.

Safety eyes: Insert 8mm eyes between Rounds 6-7 of body, about 6 sts apart. Push washers on inside firmly until they click. Pull to test secure.
Pink cheeks: With pink embroidery thread or pink yarn, make 2 small horizontal stitches just below and slightly outside each eye. 2-3 wraps.
Optional smile: Tiny black embroidered V for mouth.

Safety note: If this bat is for child under 3, embroider eyes instead of safety eyes.

7Stuff & Close - Stuff with Fiberfill, Close Opening

Stuff with fiberfill, close opening - stuff firmly but not over-stuffed that stitches stretch and show stuffing.

Stuff: Use small amounts of fiberfill at a time. Push into bottom, then sides. Body should be firm, holds shape when squeezed.
Close:
Round 11: *sc 1, dec* repeat around. [12 sts]
Round 12: dec around. [6 sts]
FO, cut 8" tail, thread through remaining 6 sts with yarn needle, pull to close, weave tail inside to hide.

8Display in Pumpkin - Your Bat is Ready for Halloween!

Your bat is ready for Halloween! Display in mini pumpkin for viral Halloween photo.

Final touches:
1. Weave in all tails: 4 sts forward, 3 back, trim.
2. Shape wings with hands - flatten and point tips.
3. Position bat in 3-4" orange pumpkin (real or crocheted). Add fairy lights, autumn leaves, cinnamon sticks for Pinterest photo.
4. Take photo from slightly above for cute effect.

Progress Checkpoints

  • Checkpoint 1 - Magic ring: Ring closed, no hole, 6 sts, marker in first st.
  • Checkpoint 2 - Body: Oval 24 sts, 5 rounds straight, no gaps, dense fabric.
  • Checkpoint 3 - Wings: 2 orange wings identical, 10 sts wide, triangular point, tails 10".
  • Checkpoint 4 - Wing attach: Symmetrical, 4-5 stitches per wing, wings stick out sideways.
  • Checkpoint 5 - Ears: 2 small black ears 6 sts, 4 sts apart on top, sewn securely.
  • Checkpoint 6 - Eyes: 8mm eyes between Rounds 6-7, 6 sts apart, washers clicked, pink cheeks embroidered.
  • Checkpoint 7 - Stuffed: Firm but not over-stuffed, opening closed with 6 sts pulled tight, no stuffing visible.
  • Checkpoint 8 - Display: Bat sits upright in pumpkin, wings visible, cute photo ready.

Troubleshooting

Can't see stitches in black yarn

Use bright light, stitch markers every 6 sts, and count out loud. Place hand under work with phone flashlight to see holes.

Wings droop down

Not sewn enough. Sew 5 sts per wing plus one extra stitch at top edge for support.

Safety eyes won't click

Push harder with flat surface behind. If still not, eyes may be too close to edge - move 1 round up.

Stuffing shows through black fabric

Hook too big or over-stuffed. Go down to 3.5mm hook or use less stuffing. Black yarn shows white stuffing more - use black stuffing or less.

Bat won't sit upright in pumpkin

Bottom not flat enough. Add extra row of sc after stuffing to create flat base, or add small flat crochet circle to bottom.

Ears floppy

Ears too big. Make smaller: MR 4 sc, sc 4, FO (no inc round).

Finishing & Display in Pumpkin

Weave all tails inside body: thread needle, go through 4 sts forward, 3 back opposite direction, trim close. For black yarn, weave in dark area to hide.

Display ideas: Mini orange pumpkin (real or crocheted) with fairy lights, autumn leaves, cinnamon sticks, small hay. Perfect for Halloween tiered tray, desk decor, or gift.

Photo tip: Shoot from slightly above, with bokeh fairy lights background - makes bat look extra cute for Pinterest/Instagram.

Care & Storage

  • Spot clean only - black yarn shows water marks if soaked.
  • If needed, hand wash cold gentle, squeeze in towel, lay flat to dry. Reshape wings while damp.
  • Store in pumpkin or display - avoid folding wings flat long-term.
  • Check safety eyes monthly if used as child's toy - ensure washers still secure.
  • Keep away from pets - small parts (eyes) could be chewed.
