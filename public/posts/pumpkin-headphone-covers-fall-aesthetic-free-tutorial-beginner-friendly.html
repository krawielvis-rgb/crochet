Why headphone covers

I live in my headphones — editing patterns, watching tutorials, walking the dog. Last October I saw a girl on the train with pumpkin earmuffs and thought "I can crochet that." First try was too small and popped off. Second was too big and flopped.

Third time I figured it out: make a flat circle that is 0.5" smaller than your ear cup, then 4 rounds straight with no increase — that creates a lip that hugs the cup. No elastic, no glue, it just stays because of the lip. I pull it off when I need to charge and pop it back on.

The ghost is optional but it's the favorite part — little white ghost with wavy bottom hanging from a white chain, bounces when you nod to music.

What you need — my fall basket

  • Yarn: Chunky / bulky — Orange 80g (my color: orange like your photo), Green 15g for stem + leaves, White 10g for ghost chain + ghost
  • Hook: 4.5mm for covers (so they stretch), 3.5mm for stem/leaves/ghost (tighter)
  • Headphones: Any over-ear — measure ear cup diameter — mine are 3.5"
  • Extras: Tapestry needle, scissors, stitch markers, small amount polyfill for pumpkin puff (optional)
If your headphones are bigger than 4", add one more increase round — go to 42 instead of 36 before straight rounds. If smaller than 3", stop at 30.

My cover in progress — orange circle becoming a cup, with green leaves waiting. This is after about 15 minutes.

How sizing works — measure your headphones

Measure across your ear cup — outside edge to outside edge. Make your circle until it is 0.5" SMALLER than that measurement — it will stretch over. For my 3.5" cups, I stop at 36 stitches = 3" circle. Then straight rounds make the sides.

Step 1: Pumpkin covers — make 2, same

STEP 1 · PUMPKIN COVERS
Cover — make 2 in orange, 4.5mm hook:
R1: mr, 6 sc (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc 1, inc] x6 (18)
R4: [sc 2, inc] x6 (24)
R5: [sc 3, inc] x6 (30)
R6: [sc 4, inc] x6 (36) — for 3.5" cup, circle ~3" — STOP HERE for standard size
For bigger 4" cup: R7: [sc 5, inc] x6 (42) — then continue
R7-R10: sc around 36 (or 42 if bigger) — 4 rounds straight, no inc — makes sides/lip (36)
R11: sl st around for clean edge, FO, leave tail

Ridge (optional but cute like pumpkin): With orange yarn and needle, wrap 6 times from center top to bottom edge, pull gently — makes pumpkin segments — knot inside

This is the main piece. R1-R6 is flat circle, R7-R10 is sides. Try it on your headphone after R8 — it should hug and not pop off. If loose, do 3 straight rounds not 4. If tight, do 5.

Step 2: Green stem + leaves — like your photo

STEP 2 · STEM + LEAVES
Stem — in green, 3.5mm:
R1: mr, 5 sc (5)
R2-R5: sc 5 — 4 rounds — little tube — FO, leave tail

Leaves — make 2, in green, 3.5mm:
Ch 7, sc in 2nd ch, hdc in next, dc in next 2, hdc in next, sc in last, ch 1, turn and work other side — sc, hdc, dc 2, hdc, sc — leaf shape — FO
Sew stem in center top of cover, leaves on sides of stem — angled like in your photo

Stem is a tiny tube — 5 stitches around for 4 rounds. Leaves are flat — ch 7 makes about 1.2" leaf. I sew them so they flop a little, not flat tight.

Pumpkin with green stem and two leaves — this is the look from your photo, stem a little curly.

Step 3: Tiny ghost charm with chain

STEP 3 · GHOST CHARM
Ghost — in white, 3.5mm:
R1: mr, 6 sc (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc 1, inc] x6 (18)
R4-R6: sc 18 — 3 rounds even (18)
R7: [sc 2, inc] x6 (24) — wavy bottom edge — sc 24
R8: sl st 24, FO, leave tail — light stuffing or none
Face: Embroider 2 black eyes between R3-R4, 4 sts apart, smile between — or use 4mm safety eyes if not for kids

Chain for hanging:
With white, ch 21, sl st in 2nd ch and each ch — 20 — makes sturdy cord — FO, sew one end to side of pumpkin cover at stem base, other end to top of ghost head

This ghost is the same as my clips collection — 6→24 wavy bottom. Chain is just ch 21, sl st back — 3" long chain so ghost bounces on shoulder like in your photo. I sew chain to side of cover, not bottom, so it hangs down side like you have it.

My ghost — wavy bottom at R7, eyes 4 stitches apart, smile small. Same ghost I use for clips.

Step 4: Assembly — how they stay on

STEP 4 · ASSEMBLY
  • Slip pumpkin covers over ear cups — lip from R7-R10 hugs cup. No glue.
  • Stem + leaves sewn on top center.
  • Ghost chain sewn to side of one cover at base of stem — I do left side, like your photo.
  • If covers slide when you take headphones off, add 2 small stitches through cover into headphone padding with matching orange — removable later.

How I wear mine

I wear them when I walk in October, at my desk, and at market. They get lots of "where did you buy those?" People love the ghost bouncing. I don't wear them in rain — chunky yarn gets heavy if wet.

If they slip or look lopsided

Cover too loose: You did 42 not 36 — or 5 straight rounds not 4. Frog one round and try again.

Circle not flat, bowl shape: You missed increases — count R1-R6: 6,12,18,24,30,36.

Ghost chain twists: Use sl st chain not just chain — sl st makes it sturdy and not curly. Or use white cotton string.

Stem flops: Stuff lightly with tiny polyfill scrap or leave empty and tack with one stitch to keep upright.

Make them yours

I made black cat version — black covers with yellow eyes, and white ghost version — white covers with orange pumpkin charms. You can also make just pumpkins without ghost for simpler look. For Christmas, make red covers with green holly leaves.
