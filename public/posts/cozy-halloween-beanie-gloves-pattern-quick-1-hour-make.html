Cozy Halloween Beanie + Gloves — The One With The Little Ghost And Pumpkin Tassels

Black beanie with two curly tassels — one ghost, one pumpkin — plus orange and black striped fingerless gloves you can finish while your coffee is still warm.

Why this set is my go-to in October

I have a lot of Halloween patterns, but this is the one I actually wear. The beanie is black so it goes with everything, and the two little charms bouncing when you walk make people smile in the grocery line. Last year the cashier asked me where I bought it and I said "I made it last night while watching Hocus Pocus" — which is true.

The gloves are fingerless because I need my fingers for my phone and for holding my mug, like in the photo. They're striped orange and black but I made the stripes thin — 2 rows each — so it doesn't look like a bee, more like cozy Halloween.

Everything is double crochet for the big pieces, single crochet for charms. No fancy stitches.

What you need

  • Yarn: Bulky / chunky — Black 120g for beanie, Orange 60g + Black 40g for gloves, White 10g ghost, Orange 10g pumpkin + green scrap for stem
  • Hook: 6.0mm for beanie and gloves, 3.5mm for charms
  • Extras: Tapestry needle, scissors, stitch marker, 2 small black beads or embroidery for ghost eyes
I use bulky so it really is 1 hour. If you use worsted, go up to 6.5mm hook or it will take longer and be smaller.

Beanie body — black double crochet, starts with ribbed band, then straight up. This is after about 20 minutes.

Sizing — how I size mine

My beanie: Adult average — 20" circumference, fits 21-22" head, slouchy. Band is ribbed so it stretches. Gloves: One size — 7.5" around hand, 7" long. If you have small hands, go down to 5.5mm hook. If large, add 4 stitches to starting chain.

Abbreviations — quick

ch — chain, sc — single crochet, dc — double crochet, inc — 2 in same, dec — 2 together, sl st — slip stitch, BLO — back loop only, FLO — front loop only, FO — fasten off. I work beanie bottom-up, in joined rounds.

Step 1: Black beanie — rib + body

STEP 1 · BEANIE
Beanie — in black, 6.0mm hook:
Ribbed band:
Ch 10, sc in 2nd ch and across 9, ch 1 turn
Row 2-60: BLO sc 9, ch 1 turn — makes ribbed band ~20" long unstretched
Join ends with sl st to form circle

Body — work along top edge of band:
R1: ch 2, dc 60 evenly around edge of band (60) — join with sl st
R2-R12: ch 2, dc 60 around (60) — 11 rounds straight — beanie ~8" tall from band
R13: [dc 4, dec dc] x10 (50)
R14: [dc 3, dec dc] x10 (40)
R15: [dc 2, dec dc] x10 (30)
R16: [dc 1, dec dc] x10 (20)
R17: dec dc x10 (10) — FO, close with needle

The ribbed band is what makes it look store-bought. I do back loop only single crochet for 60 rows — it stretches nicely. If you hate ribbing, you can do dc band but it won't be as cozy.

Beanie with spiral cords attached at ear area — this is where ghost and pumpkin will hang.

Step 2: Spiral tassel cords

STEP 2 · SPIRALS
Spiral cords — make 2:
In white for ghost side, orange for pumpkin side:
Ch 26, 3 sc in 2nd ch from hook, 3 sc in each ch across — it will curl naturally into spiral — 75 sc total — FO, leave tail
Sew one spiral on each side of beanie at about Round 4 of body, just above band

3 sc in each chain is the trick — it forces the curl. Don't block it, the curlier the better. White for ghost, orange for pumpkin — matches charms.

Step 3: Tiny ghost charm

STEP 3 · GHOST CHARM
Ghost — in white, 3.5mm hook:
R1: mr, 6 sc (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc 1, inc] x6 (18)
R4-R6: sc 18 — 3 rounds
R7: [sc 2, inc] x6 (24) — wavy edge
R8: sc 24, sl st, FO — no stuffing or tiny puff
Face: Embroider 2 black eyes between R3-R4, 4 sts apart, tiny smile, pink blush optional

Same ghost as my hair clips collection, just a touch bigger. Wavy bottom at R7 makes it float. I sew it to end of white spiral.

My tiny charms — ghosts with wavy bottoms and pumpkins with green stems. 10 minutes each.

Step 4: Tiny pumpkin charm

STEP 4 · PUMPKIN CHARM
Pumpkin — orange body, green stem, 3.5mm:
R1: mr, 6 (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: [sc 1, inc] x6 (18)
R4-R6: sc 18 — 3 rounds
R7: [sc 1, dec] x6 (12) — stuff lightly
R8: dec x6 (6) — close
Ridge: Wrap orange yarn 5 times top to bottom, pull tight for segments
Stem: Green — mr 3, sc 3 — 2 rounds, FO, sew on top with leaf — ch 3, sc, hdc

Pumpkin charms are my favorite to make — the wrap ridges make them look real. Sew to end of orange spiral.

Step 5: Striped fingerless gloves — orange and black

STEP 5 · GLOVES
Gloves — make 2, 6.0mm hook, striped:
Start orange:
Ch 24, join with sl st — careful not to twist — 24
R1-2: Orange — ch 2, dc 24 (24)
R3-4: Black — dc 24
R5-6: Orange — dc 24
R7-8: Black — dc 24
R9-10: Orange — dc 24
R11-12: Black — dc 24
R13-14: Orange — dc 24
R15: Orange — thumb hole: ch 2, dc 20, ch 4, skip 4 sts, dc 0? Actually: dc 4 to make gap — Simpler: dc 4, ch 4, skip 4 sts, dc 16 (24 counting ch)
R16-17: Black — dc 24 (working ch as sts)
R18-19: Orange — dc 24
R20: Orange — sc 24 for clean edge, FO

Stripes are 2 rows each — orange, black, orange, black. The thumb hole is just a chain skip — I tried complicated thumbs and they were annoying. This simple hole is comfy and you can still hold a mug like in the photo. If you want tighter, do sc last round.

Gloves — 2-row stripes, orange and black. Quick fingerless with thumb hole, no shaping needed.

Step 6: Assembly — attach charms

STEP 6 · ASSEMBLY

Sew ghost to end of white spiral cord, pumpkin to orange spiral. I use white yarn for ghost, orange for pumpkin, double knot inside charm. Give spiral a little tug so it bounces. Gloves need no assembly — just weave tails.

How I finish mine

I weave tails inside beanie band so they don't itch. I steam gloves lightly so stripes even out. Charms I leave unblocked so ghost waves. If beanie is too slouchy, do one less round in body (R2-R11 instead of R2-R12).

If something looks off

Beanie too tight: Band rows — you need 60 rows BLO sc 9. If you did 50, it will be tight. Add 5 rows.

Spirals not curly: You did 1 sc per ch — need 3 sc per ch to force curl.

Ghost curls up: R7 increase to 24 gives wave — if you skipped, add it.

Gloves too short: Add 2 more stripe repeats — orange 2, black 2.

Make it yours

I made one in cream beanie with black cat and ghost charms, and one in orange beanie with just ghosts. You can also add a pom pom on top plus tassels — I don't, because I like slouchy, but it's cute. If you make it, tag me — I love seeing your cozy sets.

Stitch math — checked

Beanie band BLO sc 9 x 60 = 20" rib, body dc 60 straight, decrease 60→10. Spirals ch 26 ×3 sc = 75 curl. Ghost 6→18→24 wave, pumpkin 6→18→6 + wraps. Gloves ch 24, dc stripes 2 rows each, thumb hole ch4 skip4. All counts match.
