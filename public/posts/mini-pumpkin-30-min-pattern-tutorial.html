Sizing & Gauge

Gauge: Not critical, but tight fabric holds shape. With 4mm + worsted, 6 sc = 1 inch. If stuffing shows, go down to 3.5mm.

Finished size: Pumpkin ~2.5" wide, ~2" tall. Ghost inside ~1" tall. Fits in palm like Pin.

30-min checkpoint: This pumpkin should take 30 minutes. If taking longer, your tension is too tight - relax grip, use larger hook for faster crocheting.

Abbreviations (US terms)

MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, inc = 2 sc in same st, dec = invisible decrease, st(s) = stitch(es), sl st = slip stitch, FO = fasten off

Step-by-Step Premium Pattern - Excellent Like Paid Pattern

1Magic Ring Orange

Magic ring orange - start with orange yarn, MR 6 sc, foundation with no hole for pumpkin bottom.

Round 1: With orange yarn, MR, 6 sc into ring. Pull tail to close. Place marker. [6 sts]
Round 2: inc in each st around. [12 sts]
Round 3: *sc 1, inc* repeat around. [18 sts]
Round 4: *sc 2, inc* repeat around. [24 sts]
Round 5: *sc 3, inc* repeat around. [30 sts] - Pumpkin width reached.

2Build Pumpkin Body

Build pumpkin body - simple ball shape, 5 rounds straight creates pumpkin height.

Round 6-10: sc in each st around. [30 sts] - 5 rounds straight creates 2" tall pumpkin body.
Round 11: *sc 3, dec* repeat around. [24 sts]
Round 12: *sc 2, dec* repeat around. [18 sts] - Do not stuff yet, leave for next step.
Checkpoint: Body should be round, not curling. Orange yarn shows stitches well.

3Pumpkin Ridges

Pumpkin ridges - signature technique that makes crochet pumpkin look like real pumpkin with segments.

Ridges technique (sewing through center):
After stuffing (next step), you will create ridges. Thread long orange yarn tail on yarn needle. Insert needle through center bottom, out through center top. Pull tight to indent. Wrap yarn around side and repeat through center 6 times, pulling tight each time, spacing evenly around pumpkin to create 6 ridges/segments.
Alternative crochet ridges: Work Round 6 in BLO (back loop only) to create natural ridge line.

6 ridges = realistic pumpkin like Pin. Pull tight for deep grooves.

4Stuff Pumpkin

Stuff pumpkin - stuff firmly so ridges hold shape and pumpkin is plump.

Stuff: Use small amounts fiberfill, push to bottom, sides, top. Pumpkin should be firm, holds shape when squeezed, not floppy.
Close:
Round 13: *sc 1, dec* repeat around. [12 sts]
Round 14: dec around. [6 sts] FO, close with yarn needle through 6 sts, pull to close. Leave long tail for ridges sewing.

5Green Stem

Green stem - small green stem on top, adds autumn realism like Pin middle mini pic.

Stem with green yarn + 4mm hook:
Round 1: MR, 4 sc. [4 sts]
Round 2-4: sc 4. [4 sts] - 3 rounds straight creates 0.75" stem.
Round 5: inc in each st around. [8 sts] - Creates base that flares to sew to pumpkin top.
FO, leave 8" tail.

6Green Leaf + Stem Attach

Green leaf + stem attach - crochet leaf and sew stem + leaf to top of pumpkin, like Pin.

Leaf with green yarn:
Ch 6, sc in 2nd ch, hdc, dc, hdc, sc, sl st in last ch. Other side: sc, hdc, dc, hdc, sc, sl st to first st. FO, leave tail.
Attach: Sew stem to top center of pumpkin with tail, through base 8 sts. Sew leaf to side of stem, slightly angled. Weave tails inside pumpkin.

7Cute Face

Cute face - embroider black eyes, smile, pink cheeks, kawaii style that gets clicks like Pin.

Face embroidery:
Black eyes: With black yarn and yarn needle, embroider 2 small vertical ovals or circles between Rounds 7-8, 6 sts apart. 2 wraps tall, 1 wide.
Smile: Small U-shaped smile 1 round below eyes, centered, 3 sts wide, backstitch for clean line.
Pink cheeks: With pink yarn, embroider small horizontal ovals or circles just below and outside eyes, 2 wraps, blushing cute like Pin.

Kawaii face = viral. Small eyes + big smile + pink cheeks is 2025 Pinterest trend.

8Ghost Inside Surprise - Pumpkin House!

Ghost inside surprise - tiny white ghost inside pumpkin that opens like a house, viral surprise element from Pin right mini pic!

Option A - Solid pumpkin (like main Pin image):
Pumpkin is solid with cute face, ghost is separate display idea for photos.
Option B - Pumpkin house with ghost inside (viral surprise like Pin right mini pic):
Make second pumpkin as house: Follow same pumpkin pattern but stop at Round 11, do not close. Make top as separate lid (same as bottom). Tiny ghost: White yarn MR 6→12→12 (3 rounds straight), add eyes, FO, stuff lightly. Place ghost inside bottom pumpkin, lid on top lifts off.
Tiny ghost quick pattern: MR 6, inc around [12], sc around 3 rounds [12], dec around [6], FO, add face, stuff.

Troubleshooting - Ridges, Stem, Face

Ridges not deep enough

Not pulling tight enough when sewing through center. Use strong orange yarn, pull each of 6 wraps tight until pumpkin indents deeply. Knot after last wrap.

Pumpkin looks like ball, not pumpkin shape

Need more stuffing and ridges technique. Stuff very firm, then create 6 ridges. Ridges transform ball into pumpkin.

Green stem falls off

Not sewn enough. Sew through base 8 sts twice around, weave tail inside pumpkin and knot.

Face looks scary not cute

Eyes too big or smile too big. Keep eyes small (2 wraps), smile small U 3 sts wide, pink cheeks small. Cute = small features.

Ghost inside doesn't fit

Pumpkin too small or ghost too big. Make pumpkin 30 sts (not 24), ghost 12 sts max (1" tall). Pumpkin house version needs to be larger than solid pumpkin.

30 min pattern taking longer than 30 min

Tension too tight or counting slowly. Relax grip, use 4mm hook (not 3.5mm), stitch markers to avoid counting. Experienced crocheter 30 min, beginner 45-60 min normal.

Finishing & Ghost Inside Surprise

Weave all tails inside pumpkin: 4 sts forward, 3 back opposite direction, trim close. Orange tail from ridges should be woven and knotted securely.

Care & Storage

  • Spot clean only - orange yarn may bleed if soaked.
  • If needed, hand wash cold gentle, squeeze in towel, lay flat to dry, reshape ridges while damp.
  • Store on tiered tray or display - pumpkin holds shape well.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight - orange may fade over time.
  • If pumpkin house version with ghost inside, store ghost inside so not lost.
