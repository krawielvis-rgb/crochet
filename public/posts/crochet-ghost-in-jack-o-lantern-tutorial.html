Before You Begin

This cute ghost in a jack-o-lantern is designed as the perfect first Halloween amigurumi. The ghost body is a simple oval tube - no complex shaping. The witch hat is a small cone you crochet separately and sew on. The ghost sits inside a carved pumpkin for that viral Halloween photo.

The sample in the Pin uses soft cotton yarn + 3.5mm hook, white yarn for ghost, black for witch hat, 6mm safety eyes, and fiberfill stuffing. White + black + orange pumpkin is the classic Halloween palette that performs best on Pinterest.

Why this is beginner-proof: Magic ring start closes with no hole, body is worked in continuous rounds with no joining, safety eyes go in before stuffing (easier), witch hat hides any uneven top stitches, and ruffled bottom edge forgives tension wobbles.

Materials

From your Pin - Final Result:

  • Use soft cotton yarn + 3.5mm hook: 40 yards white, 15 yards black. Worsted / medium weight. White for ghost, black for witch hat and smile embroidery.
  • 3.5mm crochet hook (E-4): Creates tight fabric so stuffing doesn't show through white yarn.
  • 6mm safety eyes (black): Pair with washers. Or embroider eyes with black yarn for child-safe version.
  • Fiberfill stuffing: Small handful.
  • Yarn needle, scissors, stitch markers: Stitch markers essential for white yarn counting.

Perfect Halloween decor! Also needs small pumpkin (real carved or crocheted) for display.

Sizing & Gauge

Gauge: Not critical for decor, but tight is better. With 3.5mm + worsted cotton, 7 sc = 1 inch. If you see stuffing through white stitches, go down to 3mm hook.

Finished size: Ghost ~2.5" tall, ~2" wide. Fits inside 4-6" mini jack-o-lantern. Witch hat ~1.5" tall.

Size checkpoint: Ghost should be slightly smaller than pumpkin opening so it peeks out cutely. If too big, use 3mm hook or fewer increase rounds.

Abbreviations (US terms)

MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, inc = 2 sc in same st, dec = invisible decrease (sc2tog), st(s) = stitch(es), sl st = slip stitch, FO = fasten off, BLO = back loop only

Complete Roadmap - 8 Steps

1. Magic Ring: White yarn, MR 6 sc, close no hole
2. Crochet the Body: Increase to 24 sts, 8 rounds straight oval
3. Add the Eyes: 6mm safety eyes between Rounds 6-7
4. Embroider Smile: Black yarn smile with yarn needle
5. Crochet the Witch Hat: Black yarn cone + brim
6. Attach the Witch Hat: Sew hat to top of ghost
7. Finish & Stuff: Stuff firmly, close with ruffled edge
8. Place in Pumpkin: Display cute ghost in jack-o-lantern!

Step-by-Step Premium Pattern - Excellent Like Paid Pattern

1Magic Ring

Start with white yarn, MR 6 sc - foundation that closes with no hole, essential for amigurumi ghost top.

Round 1: With white yarn, MR, 6 sc into ring. Pull tail to close tightly. Place marker in first st. [6 sts]
Round 2: inc in each st around. [12 sts]
Round 3: *sc 1, inc* repeat around. [18 sts]
Round 4: *sc 2, inc* repeat around. [24 sts] - Ghost head width.

Tip: White yarn shows stitches well. Pull magic ring tail firmly, weave through first round twice for security.

2Crochet the Body

Work oval body - simple tube that grows then stays straight, forms ghost shape.

Round 5-12: sc in each st around. [24 sts] - 8 rounds straight creates 2" tall ghost body.
Do not stuff yet - keep opening for eyes insertion in next step.
Checkpoint: Body should be oval, not curling. If curling, tension too tight - relax grip.

3Add the Eyes

Add eyes - 6mm safety eyes give cute expression. Position is key for cuteness.

Safety eyes placement: Between Rounds 7-8, about 6 sts apart, centered on front. Insert eyes from outside, push washers on inside firmly until click. Pull to test secure.
Child-safe alternative: Embroider eyes with black yarn - small vertical stitches 2 wraps tall.

Safety note: If ghost is for child under 3, must embroider eyes, not safety eyes.

4Embroider Smile

Embroider smile - cute U-shaped smile gives ghost personality, like Pin.

Smile embroidery: With black yarn and yarn needle, embroider small U shape 1 round below eyes, centered. Use backstitch - enter and exit same holes for clean line.
Smile size: About 3 sts wide, 1 st tall. Small smile = cuter ghost. Don't make too big or ghost looks scary not cute.

5Crochet the Witch Hat

Crochet witch hat - small black cone + brim, quick 15-minute hat that makes ghost Halloween-ready.

Witch Hat with black yarn + 3.5mm hook:
Round 1: MR, 4 sc. [4 sts]
Round 2: *sc 1, inc* twice. [6 sts]
Round 3: *sc 2, inc* twice. [8 sts]
Round 4: *sc 3, inc* twice. [10 sts]
Round 5: *sc 4, inc* twice. [12 sts] - Cone shape formed.
Round 6 (Brim): FLO, *sc 1, inc* repeat around. [18 sts] - Brim flares out.
Round 7: *sc 2, inc* repeat around. [24 sts] FO, leave 10" tail for sewing.

Brim tip: FLO = front loop only creates sharp brim edge like real witch hat.

6Attach the Witch Hat

Attach witch hat to ghost head - sewing placement makes ghost extra cute.

Attach: Position hat slightly tilted to one side (cuter than straight). With black tail and yarn needle, whip stitch around base of hat, sewing through hat brim and ghost head. Go around twice for security.
Weave ends: Pull tail inside ghost to hide.

7Finish & Stuff

Finish and stuff - stuff firmly for plump ghost, close with ruffled bottom edge like Pin.

Stuff: Use small amounts fiberfill, push to top first, then sides. Ghost should be firm, holds shape.
Ruffled bottom edge (like Pin):
Round 13: *sc 1, inc* repeat around in FLO. [36 sts] - creates ruffled wavy bottom.
Round 14: *sc 1, inc, sc 1* repeat around. [48 sts] - extra ruffle.
Close: FO, weave tail through 48 sts with yarn needle, pull lightly to close slightly but keep ruffle open. Or leave open for hollow ghost that sits over finger.

Two options: Closed ruffled ghost (stuffed) or open ghost (finger puppet style) - both work in pumpkin.

8Place in Pumpkin

Place in pumpkin - final result cute ghost in jack-o-lantern! Perfect Halloween decor photo.

Final Result - Cute Ghost in Jack-o-Lantern!
1. Weave in all tails: 4 sts forward, 3 back, trim.
2. Place ghost inside 4-6" carved pumpkin or crocheted pumpkin.
3. Add fairy lights inside pumpkin for glow, autumn leaves, small hay around.
4. Photo from slightly above with bokeh lights background - viral Pinterest style.

Progress Checkpoints

  • Checkpoint 1 - Magic ring: Closed no hole, 6 sts, marker in first st, tail 4".
  • Checkpoint 2 - Body: Oval 24 sts, 8 rounds straight, lies flat, no curling.
  • Checkpoint 3 - Eyes: 6mm eyes between Rounds 7-8, 6 sts apart, washers clicked secure.
  • Checkpoint 4 - Smile: Small U smile 3 sts wide, 1 round below eyes, centered, cute not scary.
  • Checkpoint 5 - Witch hat: Black cone 12 sts + brim 24 sts, pointed, FLO brim sharp.
  • Checkpoint 6 - Hat attach: Slightly tilted, sewn around base twice, secure.
  • Checkpoint 7 - Finish: Firmly stuffed, ruffled bottom FLO inc 36→48 sts, no stuffing showing.
  • Checkpoint 8 - Pumpkin: Ghost sits upright in pumpkin, hat visible, cute photo ready, fairy lights glow.

Troubleshooting

Magic ring hole visible at top

Didn't pull tail tight enough. Pull tail firmly, then weave through Round 1 twice in circular motion.

Safety eyes not clicking

Push harder with flat surface behind ghost. If still not, move eyes 1 round higher where fabric less thick.

Smile looks crooked

Mark smile position with pins before embroidering. Use 3 pins for 3 points of U.

Witch hat not pointy

Decreases too slow. Follow cone pattern 4→6→8→10→12 exactly - don't add extra sc rounds.

Ghost won't sit upright in pumpkin

Bottom too rounded. Ruffled edge FLO inc gives flat base. Or add small flat crochet circle to bottom.

White yarn shows stuffing

Hook too big or over-stuffed. Go down to 3mm hook, use less stuffing, or use white stuffing not brown.

Hat falls off

Not sewn enough. Sew around base twice, weave tail inside ghost and knot around a stitch inside.

Finishing & Display in Pumpkin

Weave all tails inside ghost: 4 sts forward, 3 back opposite direction, trim close. White yarn hides in white area, black tail inside hat.

Display ideas: Real carved mini pumpkin with LED fairy lights inside (safer than candle), fake crocheted pumpkin, tiered tray with autumn leaves, small hay, cinnamon sticks. Ghost peeking out of pumpkin mouth is cutest pose like Pin.

Photo tip: Shoot in evening with fairy lights glowing, slightly above angle, bokeh background - viral Halloween Pinterest style. Add text overlay "Crochet Halloween - 8-Step Tutorial".

Care & Storage

  • Spot clean only - white yarn shows dirt. Use damp cloth with mild soap.
  • If needed, hand wash cold gentle, squeeze in towel (don't wring white yarn), lay flat to dry, reshape.
  • Store in pumpkin or display year-round - ghost is cute beyond Halloween.
  • Check safety eyes monthly if child's toy - ensure washers secure.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight - white yarn can yellow over time.
