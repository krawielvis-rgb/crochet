Black Candy Corn Bandana with Orange Tip 🍬🖤

A cozy black triangle bandana with a little candy corn tip — orange, yellow, and white at the bottom — sweet for Halloween walks and pumpkin picking.

I made this one on a crisp October afternoon with candy corn candies scattered on my white wooden table — just like in your photo with little autumn leaves and acorns around. A friend asked for a bandana that was Halloween but not too bright, so I picked soft black for the main part and added that sweet candy corn tip at the bottom — orange, yellow, white — like the candy itself. I started at the bottom point and worked up, adding the colors at the end so the tip looks like a real candy corn. It took me one evening with tea, and I wore it to get apples next morning.

How this bandana is made — black triangle with candy corn tip

This bandana is a simple triangle that starts at the bottom point and gets wider each row until the top edge is about 22" wide flat — about 23" slightly stretched when you tie it — same bandana two ways — flat vs tied stretch. The main part is all black, and at the very bottom tip — the last few rows — you add orange, yellow, and white stripes so it looks like a candy corn candy. The candy corn tip is about 3" tall — orange at top of tip, yellow middle, white at very bottom point — like a real candy corn turned upside down.

It's about 14" long from top center to bottom tip — perfect for tying behind your head or around your neck — with 10" ties on each side.

Black triangle — from point to wide top

TRIANGLE — BLACK — 5.0MM — 22 INCH WIDE TOP
Black triangle — black, 5.0mm hook — starts at bottom tip — black only for now:
R1: ch 4, 3 dc in 4th chain from hook — 4 dc total — small tip — black — turn
R2: ch 3, dc in same st, dc, 2 dc in last st — 6 dc — inc 2 — black
R3: ch 3, dc same, dc 4, 2 dc last — 8 dc — black
R4: 10 dc black
R5 12 dc, R6 14, R7 16, R8 18, R9 20, R10 22, R11 24, R12 26, R13 28, R14 30, R15 32, R16 34, R17 36, R18 38, R19 40, R20 42, R21 44, R22 46, R23 48, R24 50, R25 52, R26 54 dc — by R26 top edge about 22" wide flat, 23" slightly stretched with ties — point to top center about 14" long — black only up to here — 54 dc
Continue black — R1 black tip, R2 6, R10 22, R20 42, R26 54 — inc 2 each row — count every 4 rows — same as purple bandana — easy triangle

Each row you add 2 double crochets — one at beginning, one at end — so triangle widens slowly. At row 26 you have 54 double crochets across top — about 22" wide flat on table, 23" wide when you gently stretch with ties — that extra inch is just stretch from tying. From top center down to bottom tip about 14" long.

Candy corn tip — orange, yellow, white stripes at bottom

CANDY CORN TIP — ORANGE YELLOW WHITE — 3 INCH TALL
Candy corn tip — work tip first in colors then continue black — beginner-friendly — 5.0mm:
R1 white — ch 4, 3 dc in 4th chain — 4 dc white — tip — white bottom point — turn
R2 white — ch 3, dc same, dc, 2 dc last — 6 dc white
R3 white — ch 3, dc same, dc 4, 2 dc last — 8 dc white — white section about 1" tall — white bottom point
R4 yellow — ch 3, dc same, dc 6, 2 dc last — 10 dc yellow — switch to yellow — carry black? No — cut white leave tail — start yellow
R5 yellow — 12 dc yellow
R6 yellow — 14 dc yellow — yellow section about 1" tall — yellow middle
R7 orange — 16 dc orange — switch to orange
R8 orange — 18 dc orange
R9 orange — 20 dc orange — orange section about 1" tall — orange top of candy corn tip
R10 onward black — R10 22 dc black — switch to black — R10-26 black — R10 22, R11 24, R12 26, R13 28, R14 30, R15 32, R16 34, R17 36, R18 38, R19 40, R20 42, R21 44, R22 46, R23 48, R24 50, R25 52, R26 54 dc — top 22" wide flat, 23" stretched — total length 14" — candy corn tip 3" tall at bottom — white 2 rows 4→8, yellow 3 rows 10→14, orange 3 rows 16→20, then black 22→54
Weave all tails — candy corn tip looks like real candy corn — white point, yellow middle, orange top — on black bandana — like in your photo — 3" tall tip

For the candy corn tip I start with white at the very bottom point — 4→8 dc — white point about 1" tall — then yellow 10→14 dc — yellow middle about 1" tall — then orange 16→20 dc — orange top of candy corn about 1" tall — then black from row 10 onward 22→54 dc — whole candy corn tip about 3" tall at bottom of black triangle — white at very tip, yellow middle, orange above — just like a real candy corn candy turned upside down — like in your photo where orange, yellow, white stripes are at bottom tip of black bandana.

Candy corn tip in progress — black triangle with orange, yellow, white stripes at bottom tip being crocheted — wooden hook, white yarn ball, candy corn candies, scissors and stitch markers on white wooden table — real photo — orange yellow white tip about 3" tall.

If color changes feel tricky, you can make whole triangle black 54 dc then embroider orange yellow white stripes at tip with tapestry needle — 2 rows white, 2 rows yellow, 2 rows orange at very bottom point — same look — easier for absolute beginners.

Ties and finishing

TIES — BLACK — TWO TIES — 10 INCH EACH
Ties — black, 5.0mm hook — make 2:
Ch 40, sl st in 2nd chain and each chain — 39 sl st — tie about 10" long — make 2 — sew one at each top corner of triangle — left and right top corners — for tying behind head or neck
Weave all tails — black inside black, orange inside orange, etc — 3 stitches forward 3 back — cut close

Ties are simple chains with slip stitches back — 40 chains become about 10" long tie — two ties — one on each top corner — you tie them behind head for headband or under chin for neck bandana.
