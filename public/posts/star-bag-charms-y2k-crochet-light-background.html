STAR PANEL — CHUNKY COTTON — 5MM — 3 INCH WIDE — 20 STS BASE — 5 POINTS — MAKE 2 PER STAR
Star panel — chunky cotton, 5mm hook — flat star with 5 points — make 2 panels per star:
R1: magic ring, 5 sc — 5
R2: inc 5 — 10 — 10 sts
R3: [sc, inc]5 — 15 — 15 sts
R4: [sc 2, inc]5 — 20 — 20 sts — base circle 1.5 inch wide — 20 sts — base circle for star
R5: Points — 5 points around base — *sc 4, ch 2, sl st in 2nd chain from hook (picot point), sc 3 back down same 4 sts* — first point about 0.8 inch long — repeat from * 5 times around — 5 points — each point about 0.8 inch long — star now about 3 inch wide tip to tip — flat star 3 inch wide — 20 sts base +5 points

Puffy assembly — sewing 2 panels + light stuffing — 0.8 thick

PUFFY ASSEMBLY — SEW 2 PANELS + LIGHT STUFFING — 3 INCH WIDE ×0.8 THICK PUFFY
Puffy assembly — sewing 2 star panels together:
Take 2 star panels same color — pink, yellow, or blue — 3 inch wide each — place wrong sides together — points aligned
Sew around outer edge with matching yarn and tapestry needle — whip stitch around 5 points — leave one point open — stuff lightly with polyfill — light stuffing about 5g — puffy about 0.8 inch thick — not overstuffed — soft puffy
Sew last point closed — puffy star finished — 3 inch wide ×3 tall ×0.8 thick puffy — 2 panels sewn + light stuffing — star puffy like in your photo with pink, yellow, blue puffy stars — verified
