About Sara Rain Crochet

Practical crochet help for people who want to make something by hand.

Sara Rain Crochet is an independent crochet tutorial site focused on clear, approachable patterns, beginner-friendly lessons, project ideas, and practical crochet guidance.

What you will find here

  • Step-by-step crochet tutorials for beginners and experienced makers.
  • Free crochet patterns and small projects that are practical to make.
  • Guides covering yarn, hooks, tension, stitches, finishing, and common mistakes.
  • Ideas for gifts, accessories, home projects, and seasonal crochet.

Our approach

We aim to make each tutorial useful on its own: clear materials, understandable instructions, checkpoints, finishing advice, and troubleshooting where it helps. We also explain crochet terms and techniques so readers can build skills instead of simply copying a finished project.

Independent content

The site is independently operated and is not affiliated with a yarn manufacturer, crochet hook company, marketplace, or other crochet brand unless a page explicitly says otherwise.
