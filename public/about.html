About Sara Rain Crochet

Practical crochet help for people who want to make something by hand.

Who we are

Sara Rain Crochet is an independent crochet tutorial site focused on clear, approachable patterns, beginner-friendly lessons, project ideas, and practical crochet guidance. We're here to make crochet simple, enjoyable, and accessible for everyone — whether you're picking up a hook for the first time or looking for your next creative project.

Crochet is more than a hobby — it's a way to create something special.

Our mission

We believe that everyone can learn to crochet. Our mission is to provide high-quality, easy-to-follow resources that inspire creativity, build confidence, and help people make beautiful things with their own hands.

Independent content

The site is independently operated and is not affiliated with a yarn manufacturer, crochet hook company, marketplace, or other crochet brand unless a page explicitly says otherwise.
