Welcome to my little crochet corner

Hi! I'm Sara, and this is where I share the crochet projects I make, the patterns I put together, and the tutorials I wish I had beside me when I was learning.

I love the simple feeling of sitting down with yarn and a hook and turning a few stitches into something real. Sometimes it's a small accessory, sometimes it's a cute little character, and sometimes it's a project that takes a little longer. Whatever I'm making, I want the process to feel approachable and enjoyable.

I make these tutorials to share what I'm learning and making — one stitch, one project, and one finished piece at a time.

Why I made Sara Rain Crochet

I wanted one place to put my crochet tutorials instead of keeping all my projects to myself. So I created Sara Rain Crochet as my little home on the internet — a place where I can post the things I make and where you can come back when you want to try one yourself.

You'll find beginner-friendly projects, seasonal makes, cute little crochet ideas, practical patterns, and step-by-step tutorials with pictures whenever they help make a project easier to follow.

Come crochet with me

If you found me through Pinterest, welcome! Pick a project that catches your eye, grab your yarn and hook, and follow along at your own pace. You don't have to be perfect — that's part of the fun of making something by hand.
