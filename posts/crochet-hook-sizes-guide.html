Crochet Hook Sizes Guide

Learn what the numbers on crochet hooks mean and how to choose a comfortable size for your yarn.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Step 1: Understand millimeters

The metric number printed on a hook is its diameter in millimeters. A 5 mm hook is larger than a 3.5 mm hook and generally makes larger, looser stitches with the same yarn.

Step 2: Match the yarn

Check the yarn label for a suggested hook range. Treat it as a starting point, not a rule. Your pattern may specify a different hook to achieve its gauge.

Step 3: Adjust the fabric

If your stitches are too tight, move up a hook size. If the fabric is too loose, move down. Make a small swatch whenever size matters.

Step 4: Choose comfort too

A hook that feels comfortable in your hand matters for long projects. Try ergonomic handles if your fingers become tired.
