Crochet Hook Sizes Guide

Use a controlled swatch to decide whether to go up or down a hook size for the fabric you want.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Worsted yarn
  • 5 mm hook, plus 4.5 mm and 5.5 mm if available
  • Ruler

Gauge swatch

  1. Foundation: Ch 26.
  2. Rows 1–10: Ch 1, turn. Sc across. 25 sc each row.
  3. Measure: Count stitches across 4 in / 10 cm and rows high in 4 in / 10 cm.
  4. If too tight: Repeat with the next larger hook.
  5. If too loose: Repeat with the next smaller hook.

Quick size map

Small hooks around 2–3.5 mm suit fine yarn. Medium hooks around 4–5.5 mm are common for worsted yarn. Larger hooks around 6 mm and above create more open fabric. Always follow the yarn and pattern gauge when one is provided.
