Crochet Tension Guide

Learn to control the tightness of your stitches so your projects come out closer to the size you planned.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Step 1: Relax your grip

Hold the hook firmly but do not squeeze it. Let the yarn slide through your fingers instead of pulling every loop tight.

Step 2: Make a swatch

Work a small square in the stitch used by your pattern. Count stitches across the center rather than measuring the curling edge.

Step 3: Adjust your hook

If your stitches are too small or tight, try a larger hook. If they are too large or loose, try a smaller one. Change one variable at a time.

Step 4: Keep your rhythm

Use the same hand position and yarn feed for several rows. Consistency matters more than trying to force every stitch to look identical.

Remember

Tension improves naturally with practice. If your hands hurt, take a break rather than gripping harder.
