BEGINNER BLANKET · EASY PATTERN

Crochet Blanket for Beginners

A simple lap blanket built from repeating half-double crochet rows so you can focus on even edges.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Bulky yarn, about 700–900 yd / 640–825 m
  • 6.5 mm hook
  • Needle and scissors

Finished size

Approximately 36 x 48 in / 91 x 122 cm.

Abbreviations

ch = chain · hdc = half double crochet · st = stitch

Pattern

  1. Foundation: Ch 82.
  2. Row 1: Hdc in 3rd ch from hook and across. 80 hdc.
  3. Rows 2–64: Ch 2, turn. Hdc in every st across. 80 hdc.
  4. Border: Ch 1. Sc around the blanket, working 3 sc in each corner. Join with sl st.
  5. Finish: Fasten off and weave in ends. Wash and dry according to the yarn label.

Stripe option

Change color every 8 rows. With 64 rows total, that gives you eight even stripes.
