Beginner Crochet Blanket

A relaxing blanket project built from a simple repeating row, perfect for practicing consistent tension.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 12, 2026

What you need

Choose washable worsted or chunky yarn and a hook recommended on the label. Buy enough yarn from the same dye lot before beginning.

Step 1: Start the chain

Chain a multiple of two plus one. For a small lap blanket, start around 101 chains. Keep the chain relaxed so the bottom edge does not pull tight.

Step 2: Work the repeat

Single crochet in the second chain, then alternate one single crochet and one double crochet across the row. Repeat the same rhythm on every row, turning with one chain.

Step 3: Keep the edges straight

Place the final stitch of every row in the correct turning stitch. Count the stitches occasionally. If the width changes, correct it before the blanket becomes large.

Step 4: Finish

Continue until the blanket reaches your preferred length. Fasten off, weave in the ends and gently wash according to the yarn label.
