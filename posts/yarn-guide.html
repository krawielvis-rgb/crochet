Choosing the Right Yarn

Use the label, the project and your hands to choose yarn that makes crocheting easier.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 12, 2026

Yarn choice changes the look, feel and difficulty of a project. For beginners, smooth yarn with clear stitch definition is usually the easiest place to start.

Step 1: Read the weight

Find the yarn-weight symbol on the label. DK, worsted and aran are versatile choices for learning because stitches are large enough to see without making the project painfully slow.

Step 2: Choose the fiber

Cotton is firm and useful for bags and cloths. Wool has spring and warmth for hats and garments. Acrylic is affordable, colorful and easy to wash. Match the fiber to the finished item's job.

Step 3: Check the label

Look at recommended hook size, yardage, fiber content and washing instructions. If your pattern needs multiple skeins, buy the same dye lot whenever possible.

Step 4: Test before committing

Make a small swatch. If it feels stiff, try a larger hook; if the fabric is too loose, try a smaller hook. The best yarn is the one that produces the fabric your project needs.

Do not choose yarn by color alone. Choose the fabric first, then fall in love with the color.
