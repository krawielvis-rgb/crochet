Choosing Yarn

Compare yarns with one repeatable swatch instead of guessing. This pattern is your quick yarn test.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Any two yarns you want to compare
  • Hook recommended on each yarn label
  • Ruler, scissors and needle

Abbreviations

ch = chain · sc = single crochet · dc = double crochet · st = stitch

Swatch pattern

  1. Foundation: Ch 22.
  2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and across. 21 sc.
  3. Row 2: Ch 1, turn. Sc across. 21 sc.
  4. Row 3: Ch 3, turn. Dc across. 21 dc.
  5. Rows 4–8: Alternate one sc row and one dc row.
  6. Test: Let the swatch rest flat, then measure the center 4 in / 10 cm. Record width, height, softness and drape.

How to choose

For blankets, prioritize softness and washability. For amigurumi, choose yarn that holds firm stitches. For wearables, check drape and fiber comfort before committing to a large project.
