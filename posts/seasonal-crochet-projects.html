Seasonal Crochet Projects

Let the season choose your palette and project, from light spring motifs to cozy winter accessories.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Spring

Choose fresh colors and small motifs. Crochet flowers, lightweight shawls or baskets are satisfying projects as the weather warms.

Summer

Use breathable cotton or linen blends. Market bags, coasters, bucket hats and simple tops work well for warm weather.

Autumn

Move into warm rust, cream and olive shades. Try a textured scarf, pumpkin decoration or cozy cushion cover.

Winter

Choose wool or warm blends for hats, mittens and blankets. Chunkier yarns let you finish gifts faster.

Plan your seasonal queue

Start gifts early, check the yarn care instructions and keep a small project bag ready. Seasonal crochet is more enjoyable when the deadline is not doing the crocheting for you.
