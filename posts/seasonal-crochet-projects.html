SEASONAL · 4 MINI PATTERNS

Seasonal Crochet Projects

A year-round menu of tiny projects: flower, sun coaster, leaf, and snowflake.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Spring — Flower

  1. MR, 10 sc.
  2. [Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 3, sl st] repeat 5 times around.
  3. Fasten off. 5 petals.

Summer — Sun coaster

  1. MR, 8 sc. 8 st.
  2. Inc around. 16 st.
  3. [Sc 1, inc] around. 24 st.
  4. [Ch 3, sl st in next st] around for rays. Fasten off.

Autumn — Leaf

  1. Ch 9.
  2. Sc in 2nd ch, hdc, 3 dc, hdc, 2 sc. 8 sts along one side.
  3. Turn and work into the opposite side of the chain: 2 sc, hdc, 3 dc, hdc, sc. Sl st at base.

Winter — Snowflake

  1. MR, 6 sc.
  2. [Ch 5, sl st in next sc] repeat 6 times.
  3. In each loop work [sl st, ch 3, 2 dc, ch 3, sl st]. Repeat 6 times.
  4. Fasten off and block flat.
