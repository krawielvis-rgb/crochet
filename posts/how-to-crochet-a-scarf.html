How to Crochet a Scarf

A simple ribbed scarf with a steady stitch count and a clean one-row repeat.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Worsted yarn, 450–550 yd / 410–500 m
  • 5 mm hook
  • Scissors and needle

Finished size

About 6 x 60 in / 15 x 152 cm.

Pattern

  1. Foundation: Ch 25.
  2. Row 1: Hdc in 3rd ch from hook and across. 23 hdc.
  3. Rows 2–110: Ch 2, turn. Work hdc in the back loop only across. 23 hdc each row.
  4. Final row: Hdc normally across. 23 hdc.
  5. Finish: Fasten off, weave ends and lightly block.

Length check

Measure after every 20 rows. Stop at 54–60 in / 137–152 cm if you prefer a shorter scarf.
