How to Crochet a Scarf

Make a comfortable everyday scarf with a simple row repeat that lets you crochet without constantly checking a pattern.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

What you need

Choose a soft DK or worsted yarn and a hook that suits the yarn. A smooth fiber makes the repeating stitches easy to see.

Step 1: Choose the width

Chain until the foundation is roughly the width you want. For a typical adult scarf, a starting chain around 25–35 stitches is a useful range, depending on yarn and stitch.

Step 2: Work the first row

Single crochet in the second chain from the hook and across. Chain one and turn.

Step 3: Repeat

For a simple textured scarf, alternate single crochet and double crochet across every row. Continue until the scarf reaches your desired length.

Step 4: Finish

Fasten off, weave in both ends and gently block the scarf if you want straighter edges. Wash according to the yarn label.
