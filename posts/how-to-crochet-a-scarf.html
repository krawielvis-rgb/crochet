How to crochet a simple scarf

A wearable first project that teaches even tension over a long stretch.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 11, 2026

A scarf is a wonderful bridge project, it is simple enough for an early beginner, but long enough to genuinely build your tension consistency over dozens of rows.

Pick a stitch with a little texture

Plain single crochet works, but a half double crochet or a simple shell stitch gives a scarf more drape and visual interest without adding much difficulty.

Width and length that actually work

A practical scarf is usually 6 to 8 inches wide and 60 inches long. Chain a foundation row matching your desired width, then simply keep working rows until you reach your target length, no shaping required.

Choose a soft, drapey yarn

A stiff, structured yarn that is perfect for a coaster will feel like cardboard around your neck. Look for something advertised as soft or for garments, a wool or wool blend drapes especially well.

A scarf is just consistency, worn around your neck.

Fringe is an easy, forgiving finishing touch

If your edges are not perfectly even, a simple fringe along both short ends hides a multitude of small inconsistencies while giving the piece a finished, intentional look.
