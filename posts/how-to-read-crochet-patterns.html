How to Read Crochet Patterns

Learn the language of a written pattern by making a tiny swatch with repeats, brackets and row counts.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Abbreviations

ch = chain · sc = single crochet · dc = double crochet · sk = skip · rep = repeat · st = stitch

Practice pattern

  1. Foundation: Ch 16.
  2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc across. 15 sc.
  3. Row 2: Ch 1, turn. [Sc 1, dc 1] across, ending with sc. 15 sts.
  4. Row 3: Ch 1, turn. Sc across. 15 sc.
  5. Row 4: Ch 3, turn. Dc across. 15 dc.
  6. Rows 5–8: Repeat Rows 2–4, then Row 2 once more.

How to decode it

Numbers at the end tell you how many stitches should exist. Parentheses or brackets group a repeat. A row range such as “Rows 5–8” tells you exactly which rows to repeat.
