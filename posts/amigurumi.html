AMIGURUMI · BEGINNER PATTERN

Amigurumi

Make a tiny stuffed ball that can become a bear, chick, ornament or keychain base.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Sport or worsted cotton yarn, about 20 yd / 18 m
  • 3.5 mm hook
  • Polyester stuffing and tapestry needle
  • Optional: safety eyes

Abbreviations

MR = magic ring · sc = single crochet · inc = 2 sc in one st · dec = sc two together · st = stitch

Pattern

  1. Round 1: MR, 6 sc. 6 st.
  2. Round 2: Inc around. 12 st.
  3. Round 3: [Sc 1, inc] around. 18 st.
  4. Round 4: [Sc 2, inc] around. 24 st.
  5. Rounds 5–8: Sc around. 24 st each.
  6. Round 9: [Sc 2, dec] around. 18 st.
  7. Round 10: [Sc 1, dec] around. 12 st.
  8. Stuff: Fill firmly but do not stretch the stitches.
  9. Round 11: Dec around. 6 st. Fasten off and close the opening.

Finishing

Add embroidered details or eyes before closing if needed. Keep the stuffing even so the finished shape stays round.
