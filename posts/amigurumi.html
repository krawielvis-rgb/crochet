Cute Amigurumi

Create a tiny stuffed crochet friend using simple rounds, increases, decreases and a little patience.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 12, 2026

Amigurumi is crocheted tightly in continuous rounds so stuffing cannot easily show through. Start with a simple ball shape before attempting complicated animals.

Step 1: Start the sphere

Make a magic ring and crochet six single crochets into it. Pull the ring closed. Work two single crochets into every stitch on the next round to create six increases.

Step 2: Build the body

Continue increasing evenly until the circle reaches the width you want. Then crochet several rounds with no increases. Keep your stitches tight and use a stitch marker to find the start of each round.

Step 3: Shape and stuff

Begin decreasing by working two stitches together at regular intervals. Add small amounts of polyester stuffing before the opening becomes too small. Firm stuffing gives the toy a neat shape.

Step 4: Close and decorate

Finish the final decreases, cut the yarn and pull the tail through. Use a tapestry needle to close the remaining opening. Add embroidered eyes, a tiny nose, ears or other details securely.

Make your next friend

Once you can make a sphere, you can turn the same technique into bears, bunnies, fruits and miniature ornaments by changing the proportions.
