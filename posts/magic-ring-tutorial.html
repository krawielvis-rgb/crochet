Magic Ring Tutorial

Make a secure adjustable center for crochet circles without leaving a hole.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Step 1: Form the loop

Wrap the yarn around two fingers, leaving a tail. Cross the working yarn over the tail to create a loop and hold the crossing point firmly.

Step 2: Insert the hook

Slide your hook under the first strand of the loop, grab the working yarn and pull up a loop. Chain one to secure the ring.

Step 3: Crochet into the ring

Work the number of stitches your pattern requires directly around the loop and over the yarn tail. Keep the stitches relaxed enough to move the hook.

Step 4: Close it

Hold the stitches, then pull the yarn tail firmly. The center will cinch closed. Finish with a slip stitch if the pattern calls for a closed round.

Secure the tail

Weave the tail through several stitches on the back before trimming. A secure tail keeps the center from opening with use.
