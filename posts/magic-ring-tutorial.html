TECHNIQUE · MAGIC RING

Magic Ring Tutorial

Practice making a tight adjustable center, then turn it into a flat four-round circle.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Worsted yarn, about 25 yd / 23 m
  • 5 mm hook

Abbreviations

MR = magic ring · sc = single crochet · inc = 2 sc in one stitch · sl st = slip stitch

Pattern

  1. Round 1: Make an adjustable ring. Ch 1, 6 sc into ring. Pull tail to close. Sl st to first sc. 6 st.
  2. Round 2: Inc in every st. 12 st.
  3. Round 3: [Sc 1, inc] around. 18 st.
  4. Round 4: [Sc 2, inc] around. 24 st.
  5. Finish: Sl st, fasten off and weave the tail through the center and back of several stitches.

Tip

Keep the working yarn behind the ring while making the first stitches. Pull the center tail firmly only after all six first-round stitches are complete.
