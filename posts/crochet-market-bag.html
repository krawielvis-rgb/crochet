How to crochet a market bag

A sturdy, reusable project that earns its keep every trip to the store.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 11, 2026

A crochet market bag is one of the most genuinely useful things you can make, and unlike a lot of first projects, it gets used constantly rather than displayed once and forgotten.

Choose a sturdy yarn

Cotton or a cotton-blend worsted weight yarn is standard here. It needs to hold real weight, produce and groceries are not light, so avoid anything too soft or stretchy for the main body.

Build the base first

Most market bags start with an oval or circular base, worked flat, before turning to work up the sides in the round. A firmer stitch, single or half double crochet, keeps the base from sagging under weight.

The sides can be more open

A mesh or filet pattern for the bag's sides reduces weight and lets the bag compress down small when empty, a nice practical feature for something you will carry folded in a purse or car.

A bag you actually use every week is worth more than a decoration you use once.

Reinforce the straps

Straps take the most stress of any part of the bag. Work them a bit thicker than you might think necessary, or crochet over a length of cord for extra strength, a strap that stretches or snaps ruins an otherwise great bag.
