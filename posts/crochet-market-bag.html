Crochet Market Bag

Make a lightweight reusable bag with an open mesh body and reinforced handles.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

What you need

Choose sturdy cotton yarn, a suitable hook, scissors and a tapestry needle.

Step 1: Make the base

Chain 30 and single crochet around the chain, placing extra stitches at both ends. Continue around until the base is the size you want.

Step 2: Build the mesh

Chain five, skip two stitches and single crochet into the next. Repeat around. On the next round, work into the chain spaces. Continue until the bag body is tall enough.

Step 3: Reinforce the top

Work one or two rounds of single crochet around the opening. This gives the handles a stronger edge.

Step 4: Add handles

Mark two opposite handle positions. Chain the handle length you want and attach it to the opposite marker with sturdy stitches. Repeat for the second handle and weave the ends securely.
