Crochet Market Bag

A reusable open-mesh bag with a firm base and simple repeated mesh rounds.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Worsted cotton, about 250 yd / 230 m
  • 4.5 mm hook
  • Needle and scissors

Abbreviations

ch = chain · sc = single crochet · dc = double crochet · sl st = slip stitch · sp = space

Pattern

  1. Base: Ch 31. Sc in 2nd ch and across. 30 sc.
  2. Rounds 2–6: Ch 1, turn. Sc across. 30 sc. Fold base and continue around its long edges with sc to form a rectangle.
  3. Mesh Round: Ch 5, skip 2 sts, sc in next. Repeat around. Join with sl st.
  4. Mesh Rounds 2–18: Ch 5, sc in each ch-5 space around. Join. Open mesh.
  5. Top: Sc around twice, working 1 sc in each space and 1 sc over each join.
  6. Handles: Mark 8 stitches on each side. Sc to marker, ch 35, skip 8 sts, sc to next marker. Repeat for second handle. Sc one final round around, including 35 sc across each handle chain.
  7. Finish: Fasten off and reinforce handle joins.
