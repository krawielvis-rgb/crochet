Crochet Stitch Abbreviations

Keep this quick reference nearby whenever a crochet pattern starts looking like a secret code.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Single crochet — sc

Insert the hook, yarn over, pull up a loop, then yarn over and pull through both loops.

Half double crochet — hdc

Yarn over, insert the hook and pull up a loop. Yarn over again and pull through all three loops.

Double crochet — dc

Yarn over, insert the hook and pull up a loop, then work through two loops twice.

Slip stitch — sl st

Insert the hook, yarn over and pull the yarn directly through the stitch and the loop on the hook.

Read the pattern's key

Abbreviations vary between designers and US/UK terminology differs, so always check the pattern's own glossary before starting.
