REFERENCE · STITCH SAMPLER

Crochet Stitch Abbreviations

Use this small sampler to practice the abbreviations you see in modern written crochet patterns.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Abbreviations

ch = chain · sc = single crochet · hdc = half double crochet · dc = double crochet · sl st = slip stitch · st = stitch · inc = increase · dec = decrease

Sampler

  1. Foundation: Ch 17.
  2. Row 1: Sc across. 16 sc.
  3. Row 2: Hdc across. 16 hdc.
  4. Row 3: Dc across. 16 dc.
  5. Row 4: Sl st across. 16 sl st.
  6. Row 5: Sc across, placing one inc in the center. 17 sc.
  7. Row 6: Sc 7, dec, sc 8. 16 sc.

Reading repeats

Square brackets group stitches to repeat. “Around” means repeat until you reach the round marker or beginning point. Always check the stated stitch count before continuing.
