FLOWER PATTERN · 5 PETALS

Crochet Flower

A quick five-petal flower for hats, bags, blankets, cards and gift wrapping.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Small amount of worsted yarn in 2 colors
  • 4 mm hook
  • Scissors and needle

Finished size

About 3 in / 7.5 cm across.

Abbreviations

MR = magic ring · ch = chain · sc = single crochet · dc = double crochet · sl st = slip stitch

Pattern

  1. Round 1: MR, ch 1, 10 sc in ring. Sl st to first sc. 10 sc.
  2. Round 2: [Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 3, sl st in next sc] repeat 5 times. 5 petals.
  3. Round 3: Join new color behind the petals. [Sc in next sc, ch 4] repeat 5 times, sl st to first sc to form 5 loops.
  4. Round 4: In each loop work [sc, 3 dc, sc]. Repeat around. 5 petals.
  5. Finish: Sl st to first sc, fasten off and weave ends.

Make it your own

Use a 3 mm hook for a smaller flower or a 5 mm hook and thicker yarn for a larger applique.
