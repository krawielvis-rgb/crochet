FLOWER PATTERN · 5 PETALS

Easy Crochet Flower Pattern

A quick five-petal crochet flower for hats, bags, blankets, cards, gift wrapping, and other small handmade projects.

Sara Rain Crochet · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Small amount of worsted or DK yarn in 2 colors
  • 4 mm crochet hook
  • Scissors and yarn needle

Finished size

About 3 in / 7.5 cm across, depending on yarn and tension.

Abbreviations

MR = magic ring · ch = chain · sc = single crochet · dc = double crochet · sl st = slip stitch

Pattern

  1. Round 1: Make a magic ring, ch 1, then work 10 sc into the center. Sl st to the first sc. 10 sc.
  2. Round 2: For each petal, work ch 3, 2 dc, ch 3, then sl st into the next stitch. Repeat until 5 petals are formed.
  3. Round 3: Join the second color behind the petals. Use the center stitches to create a neat base for the next layer if you want a two-color flower.
  4. Round 4: In each available loop work [sc, 3 dc, sc]. Repeat around for fuller outer petals.
  5. Finish: Sl st to the starting point, fasten off, and weave the ends securely.

Stitch-count checkpoint

You should have five visually similar petals. If you get four or six, check the skipped stitch between petals and make sure the center round has 10 sc.

Make it your own

Use a 3 mm hook for a smaller flower or a 5 mm hook and thicker yarn for a larger applique. Try the flower as a bag charm, zipper pull, hair accessory, blanket motif, or gift decoration.
