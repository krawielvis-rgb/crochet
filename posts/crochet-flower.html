Easy Crochet Flower Tutorial

Make a simple six-petal crochet flower that works beautifully as an embellishment, gift topper, or small motif.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 12, 2026

This small flower is a perfect confidence-building project: it uses basic stitches, works quickly, and can turn a scrap of yarn into something useful.

What you will need

  • A crochet hook that suits your yarn
  • A small amount of smooth yarn
  • Scissors and a tapestry needle

Step 1: Make the centre

Make a magic ring, then work six single crochets into the ring. Pull the ring closed and slip stitch into the first stitch. If you prefer, chain two and work into the second chain instead.

Step 2: Form the petals

Chain two, then work a small cluster of double crochet stitches into the next stitch or space. Finish the petal with a slip stitch into the following stitch. Repeat around the centre until you have six petals.

Step 3: Shape the flower

Gently spread the petals with your fingers. If the flower curls too much, loosen your tension on the next one or use a slightly larger hook.

Step 4: Finish neatly

Cut the yarn, pull the tail through, and weave both ends into the back of the flower. Trim only after the ends are secure.

Easy ways to use it

Sew a flower onto a hat, bag, cardigan, gift wrap, or hair accessory. Make several in different colors and join them into a small garland or decorative cluster.

Small motifs are where crochet becomes wonderfully addictive.
