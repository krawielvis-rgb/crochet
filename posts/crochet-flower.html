Easy Crochet Flower Tutorial — Step by Step

Make a cheerful six-petal crochet flower with a few basic stitches. It is quick, beginner-friendly, and lovely for gifts, accessories, cards, bags, and home decor.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 11, 2026

This little flower is a great first crochet motif because you only need a few familiar stitches. Make one in a single color for a clean look, or use a contrasting center to make the petals stand out.

What you will need

  • Small amount of worsted-weight yarn in your main color
  • A small amount of contrasting yarn for the center, optional
  • 5 mm crochet hook
  • Scissors
  • Tapestry needle

Stitches used

  • ch — chain
  • sl st — slip stitch
  • sc — single crochet
  • dc — double crochet

Step 1: Make the center ring

Make a magic ring. If you prefer, chain 4 and join with a slip stitch to form a small ring.

Chain 1, then work 12 sc into the ring. Pull the center closed and join to the first sc with a slip stitch.

Step 2: Make the first petal

Slip stitch into the next stitch. Chain 2, then work 2 dc into the same stitch. Chain 2 and slip stitch into that same stitch.

You have made your first rounded petal.

Step 3: Repeat around the flower

Repeat the same sequence around the ring: slip stitch into the next stitch, chain 2, work 2 dc into that stitch, chain 2, then slip stitch into the same stitch.

Continue until you have 6 petals. Join with a slip stitch and fasten off.

Tip: Keep your stitches relaxed. If the petals feel stiff or difficult to shape, loosen your tension slightly.

Step 4: Finish the flower

Cut the yarn, leaving a short tail, and pull it through the final loop. Use your tapestry needle to weave the ends securely into the back of the flower.

Gently flatten the petals with your fingers. You can leave the center as-is or add a small contrasting button or embroidered detail.

Easy ways to use your flower

  • Sew it onto a tote bag or crochet basket.
  • Add it to a hat, headband, or scarf.
  • Make several flowers for a garland.
  • Use one as a gift topper or handmade card decoration.
  • Join flowers together for a colorful crochet motif.

Make it your own

Try different yarn colors and hook sizes to change the flower's character. A smaller hook and thinner yarn will create a delicate flower, while thicker yarn will make a chunkier statement piece.
