Crochet Gift Ideas

Small handmade gifts feel personal because they carry your time as well as your craft.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

1. Make a useful coaster set

Crochet four or six cotton coasters in the recipient's favorite colors. Stack them with a simple ribbon for an easy presentation.

2. Try a flower bookmark

Make a narrow chain, add a small flower at one end and weave in the tails. It is quick enough to personalize for several readers.

3. Crochet a mug cozy

Measure around the mug, crochet a rectangle in single crochet and add a button loop. Leave the cozy slightly loose so it slides on easily.

4. Make a mini pouch

Crochet two matching rectangles, join three sides and add a drawstring or button. Use sturdy cotton for everyday use.

5. Add a handmade finishing touch

Even a purchased gift becomes personal with a crochet flower, bow or small tag. Choose colors that match the recipient rather than your own stash.
