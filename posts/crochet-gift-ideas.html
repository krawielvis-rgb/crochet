10 quick crochet gift ideas

Small, finishable projects for when the deadline is closer than the skill level.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 11, 2026

Not every gift needs to be a week-long blanket. These are projects that a confident beginner can realistically finish in a single weekend.

Ten ideas worth trying

  • A set of simple coasters in a coordinating color palette
  • A small amigurumi keychain animal
  • A market tote bag in sturdy cotton
  • A pair of fingerless mitts
  • A catnip toy for a cat-owning friend
  • A headband in a soft worsted yarn
  • A dishcloth set, more useful than it sounds
  • A small drawstring pouch
  • A phone or tablet sleeve
  • A simple baby hat, sized for newborn or 0-3 months

Match the project to how much time you actually have

Coasters and keychains can be finished in an evening. A tote bag or hat is a realistic single-weekend project. Save the mitts or headband for when you have two free weekends, ribbing and shaping take longer than flat pieces.

A handmade gift says someone spent hours thinking of you, one stitch at a time.

Whatever you choose, start it earlier than you think you need to, first attempts at a new pattern almost always take longer than expected.
