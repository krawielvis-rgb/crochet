GIFTS · 3 MINI PATTERNS

Crochet Gift Ideas

Three fast handmade gifts with small yarn requirements and simple stitch counts.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

1. Mini coaster

  1. MR, 6 sc. 6 st.
  2. Inc around. 12 st.
  3. [Sc 1, inc] around. 18 st.
  4. Sc around, fasten off. 18 st.

2. Simple bookmark

  1. Ch 7.
  2. Sc in 2nd ch and across. 6 sc.
  3. Rows 2–18: Ch 1, turn, sc across. 6 sc.
  4. Fasten off; add a tassel if desired.

3. Flower applique

  1. MR, 10 sc; sl st.
  2. [Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 3, sl st in next sc] repeat 5 times.
  3. Fasten off and weave ends.

Gift finish

Use matching yarn for all three pieces, attach a handwritten tag and block flat before wrapping.
