Crochet vs Knitting

A hands-on sampler for comparing crochet fabric with the loop structure you see in knitted fabric.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Crochet sampler

  1. Foundation: Ch 21.
  2. Rows 1–5: Sc across. 20 sc.
  3. Rows 6–10: Hdc across. 20 hdc.
  4. Finish: Sc around the edge and fasten off.

Compare

Crochet builds fabric one active loop at a time, so it is easy to shape, join and undo. Knitting commonly keeps many live loops on needles and excels at stretchy, continuous fabrics. Neither is “better”; the project decides which structure fits.

Pattern choice

Choose crochet when you want quick shaping, sturdy motifs or one-hook portability. Choose knitting when a soft, highly elastic fabric is the priority.
