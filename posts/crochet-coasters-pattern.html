Crochet Coasters

A sturdy round coaster designed to lie flat and finish in a few short rounds.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Cotton yarn, about 25 yd / 23 m
  • 4 mm hook

Pattern

  1. Round 1: MR, 8 sc. 8 st.
  2. Round 2: Inc around. 16 st.
  3. Round 3: [Sc 1, inc] around. 24 st.
  4. Round 4: [Sc 2, inc] around. 32 st.
  5. Round 5: Sc around. 32 st.
  6. Round 6: [Sc 3, inc] around. 40 st.
  7. Finish: Sl st, fasten off and weave ends.

Make a set

Repeat the pattern four times using two colors. Block the coasters flat and let them dry completely before use.
