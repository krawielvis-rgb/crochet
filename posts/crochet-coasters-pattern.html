Crochet Coasters Pattern

Turn a little cotton yarn into a useful coaster while practicing increases and clean round joins.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 12, 2026

What you need

Use cotton yarn, a hook suited to it, scissors and a tapestry needle. Cotton is ideal because it handles moisture and heat better than many soft fibers.

Step 1: Make the center

Create a magic ring and crochet eight single crochets into it. Pull the ring closed and slip stitch to the first stitch.

Step 2: Increase the circle

Work two single crochets in each stitch for round two. On the next round, alternate one single crochet and two single crochets. Keep increases evenly distributed so the coaster stays flat.

Step 3: Add a neat edge

Work one final round of single crochet. If you want a decorative finish, try slip stitches or small scallops around the outside.

Step 4: Finish

Fasten off and weave the tail through several stitches on the back. Make four or six coasters in coordinating colors for an easy handmade set.
