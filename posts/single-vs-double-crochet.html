Single vs Double Crochet

Two essential stitches can create very different fabrics. Learn the difference by making them side by side.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Single crochet

Insert the hook, yarn over and pull up a loop. Yarn over again and pull through both loops. Single crochet makes a compact, sturdy fabric.

Double crochet

Yarn over before inserting the hook. Pull up a loop, yarn over and pull through two loops, then yarn over and pull through the remaining two. Double crochet is taller and grows faster.

Step 1: Make a test chain

Chain 16. Work one row entirely in single crochet, then make another sample in double crochet. Keep the tension similar in both swatches.

Step 2: Compare the fabric

Single crochet is dense and firm, making it useful for bags and amigurumi. Double crochet is lighter and drapes more, making it useful for blankets, scarves and garments.

Step 3: Practice switching

Make rows that alternate the two stitches. Notice how the height changes. This exercise helps you recognize stitches when reading a pattern.
