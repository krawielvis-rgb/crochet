How to Fix Crochet Mistakes

Most crochet mistakes are easier to repair than they look. Learn when to undo, when to drop a stitch and when to simply keep going.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Step 1: Count before guessing

Compare your stitch count with the pattern. Too few stitches usually means a missed stitch; too many often means an accidental increase.

Step 2: Find the last correct row

Place a marker at the point where the work was correct. Undo one stitch at a time until you reach that point instead of pulling out an entire project blindly.

Step 3: Fix common problems

For a missed stitch, gently unravel back and remake it. For a twisted chain, undo to the twist. For a split yarn loop, pull it back through carefully and remake the stitch.

Step 4: Prevent repeats

Use a stitch marker, count every few rows and read the pattern one instruction at a time. Good lighting makes a surprising difference.

Frogging is not failure; it is part of making.
