Your First Crochet Project

A real numbered pattern: make a simple 4 in / 10 cm practice square while learning the basic rhythm of crochet.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Worsted-weight yarn, about 35 yd / 32 m
  • 5 mm (H/8) crochet hook
  • Scissors and tapestry needle

Finished size

Approximately 4 x 4 in / 10 x 10 cm. Gauge is not critical; keep your tension even.

Abbreviations

ch = chain · sc = single crochet · st = stitch · sl st = slip stitch

Pattern

Foundation: Ch 17.

  1. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. 16 sc.
  2. Row 2: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. 16 sc.
  3. Rows 3–16: Repeat Row 2. Count 16 stitches after every row.
  4. Border: Ch 1. Sc evenly around the square, working 3 sc in each corner. Sl st to first sc.
  5. Finish: Fasten off, leave a 6 in / 15 cm tail and weave in both ends.

Pattern notes

If the edges grow or shrink, count your 16 stitches before starting the next row. The small square is your first gauge lesson and can become a coaster or practice swatch.
