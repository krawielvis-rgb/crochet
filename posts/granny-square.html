Classic Granny Square

Build the timeless crochet motif one round at a time, then turn it into blankets, bags, cushions or anything you can imagine.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 12, 2026

The granny square is made from groups of three double crochets separated by corners. Once you understand that rhythm, the pattern becomes wonderfully predictable.

What you need

  • Worsted-weight yarn in 2–4 colors
  • A 5 mm crochet hook
  • Scissors and a tapestry needle

Step 1: Create the center

Make a magic ring. Chain three, then work two double crochets into the ring. Chain two. Repeat the three-double-crochet cluster and chain-two corner three more times. Join with a slip stitch.

Step 2: Start round two

Slip stitch into the nearest corner space. Chain three, work two double crochets into that space, chain two, then three more double crochets in the same corner. Chain one and move to the next corner.

Step 3: Grow the square

In every corner work three double crochets, chain two and three double crochets. Along each side, place a three-double-crochet cluster into the open space. Continue around and join the round.

Step 4: Change colors

Fasten off at the end of a round and join a new color in a corner space. Keep the tails long enough to weave in securely. Try a light center with progressively darker outer rounds for extra definition.

Make it bigger

Repeat rounds until your square reaches the size you want. Make several matching squares and join them through the back loops for a classic blanket or cushion.
