Crochet vs Store Bought

Sometimes handmade is the perfect choice, and sometimes buying ready-made makes more sense. Here is how to decide.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Time versus convenience

A shop-bought item wins when you need something immediately. Crochet takes time, but that time can be part of the value if you enjoy making.

Customization

Handmade wins when size, color and texture matter. You can adjust a crochet piece to a specific person, room or wardrobe.

Cost

Yarn, tools and your time all have value. Compare the full material cost rather than assuming handmade is automatically cheaper.

Durability and repair

A handmade item can be repaired and modified. Choose sturdy yarn and good finishing when you expect frequent use.

The best answer

You do not have to choose one forever. Buy when convenience matters and crochet when the process or personalization is the point.
