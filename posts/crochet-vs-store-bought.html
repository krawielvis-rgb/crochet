Crochet vs Store Bought

Use one repeatable mini project to compare materials, time, durability and personalization.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Mini project

  1. Round 1: MR, 8 sc. 8 st.
  2. Round 2: Inc around. 16 st.
  3. Round 3: [Sc 1, inc] around. 24 st.
  4. Round 4: [Sc 2, inc] around. 32 st.
  5. Finish: Fasten off and weave ends.

Record the result

Write down yarn cost, minutes spent, finished size and how easy it is to repair. Then compare those values with a similar purchased item. Handmade value is not only price: fit, color, customization and the pleasure of making count too.
