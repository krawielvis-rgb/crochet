How to Crochet in the Round

Build circles, hats and tiny shapes from a center point without losing track of your rounds.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Step 1: Start the center

Make a magic ring and work six single crochets into it. Pull the tail to close the hole. Place a marker in the first stitch.

Step 2: Increase evenly

Work two stitches into each stitch on round two. On round three, alternate one single crochet with an increase. Even spacing keeps the circle flat.

Step 3: Join or spiral

For joined rounds, slip stitch to the first stitch and use the turning chain required by your pattern. For continuous rounds, simply begin the next stitch and move the marker each round.

Step 4: Check the shape

If the circle cups, add increases. If it ruffles, there are probably too many. Adjust the next round rather than forcing the fabric flat.

Where to use it

This technique is the foundation for hats, baskets, flowers, mandalas and amigurumi. Once the rhythm feels natural, you can build almost any shape from it.
