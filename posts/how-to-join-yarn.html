How to Join Yarn

Switch colors or start a fresh skein without leaving a bulky knot in your crochet.

Words by Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Step 1: Stop near the end

When only a short length remains, work the final stitch until two loops are left on your hook.

Step 2: Add the new yarn

Place the new yarn over the hook and pull it through those final loops. Leave tails from both yarns hanging at least 10–15 cm.

Step 3: Continue

Keep crocheting with the new yarn. For the next few stitches, maintain normal tension so the join does not become tighter than the surrounding fabric.

Step 4: Weave the tails

Use a tapestry needle to weave each tail in several directions on the wrong side. Trim only after the yarn is secure.

Color changes

The same technique works for stripes. For a cleaner stripe, change colors at the final step of the stitch before the new row or round begins.
