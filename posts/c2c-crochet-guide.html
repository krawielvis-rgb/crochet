C2C Crochet Guide

Build a pixel-style fabric from tiny diagonal blocks and turn simple color charts into blankets, scarves and wall art.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 12, 2026

Corner-to-corner crochet grows diagonally rather than in straight rows. Each little block is made from three double crochets, making the technique ideal for geometric designs.

Step 1: Make the first block

Chain six. Double crochet into the fourth chain from the hook, then into each of the next two chains. Turn your work. This three-double-crochet group is your first block.

Step 2: Increase

Chain six and make another block. Slip stitch into the chain-three space of the previous block, then chain three and make three double crochets in that space. Repeat to add blocks across the diagonal.

Step 3: Start decreasing

When the project reaches its widest point, stop adding blocks. Turn and slip stitch across the last three stitches to reach the next chain space, then build the next block. Each diagonal becomes one block shorter.

Step 4: Change colors cleanly

Finish the last double crochet of a block with the new color, then carry or weave the old color along the edge when practical. Keep unused yarn tidy so it does not create bulky floats.

Reading a C2C chart

Each square on a chart usually represents one C2C block. Work diagonally through the chart and count blocks rather than individual stitches.
