C2C · MINI PATTERN

C2C Crochet Guide

Practice corner-to-corner construction with a tiny four-color diagonal swatch.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Worsted yarn in 2 colors
  • 5 mm hook
  • Needle and scissors

Abbreviations

ch = chain · dc = double crochet · sl st = slip stitch

Mini C2C

  1. Tile 1: Ch 6, dc in 4th, 5th and 6th chains. 1 tile.
  2. Row 2: Ch 6, dc in 4th–6th ch. Sl st into previous tile, ch 3, 3 dc in ch-3 space. 2 tiles.
  3. Row 3: Repeat the increase sequence across. 3 tiles.
  4. Row 4: Repeat the increase sequence across. 4 tiles.
  5. Row 5: Work the decrease sequence until 3 tiles remain, then continue decreasing to 1 tile.
  6. Finish: Fasten off and weave ends.

Color changes

Change color at the slip-stitch join before beginning the next tile. Keep unused yarn at the back and weave ends securely.
