MATERIALS · AMIGURUMI TEST

Best Yarn for Amigurumi

Test a yarn for stitch definition, firmness and stuffing coverage before using it for a full toy.

Crochet Journal · Updated September 12, 2026

Materials

  • Cotton or cotton-blend yarn
  • Hook 0.5–1 mm smaller than the yarn label suggestion
  • Small amount of stuffing

Test ball

  1. Round 1: MR, 6 sc. 6 st.
  2. Round 2: Inc around. 12 st.
  3. Round 3: [Sc 1, inc] around. 18 st.
  4. Round 4: [Sc 2, inc] around. 24 st.
  5. Rounds 5–7: Sc around. 24 st.
  6. Rounds 8–10: [Sc 2, dec] around, then stuff before closing.
  7. Finish: Dec around, fasten off and close.

Choose the winner

Pick the yarn that gives small, even stitches, minimal gaps and a firm shape without making your hands uncomfortable.
