AMIGURUMI GUIDE · 6 MIN READ

Best Yarn for Amigurumi

Choose yarn that keeps tiny stitches crisp, survives stuffing and makes shaping easier.

Words by Crochet Journal • Updated September 12, 2026

Amigurumi benefits from yarn that is smooth, strong and easy to see. The right choice makes increases, decreases and small details much easier to control.

Step 1: Pick a smooth fiber

Mercerized or regular cotton is a popular choice because it has clear stitch definition and little stretch. Acrylic can also work well when you want a softer, inexpensive toy.

Step 2: Choose the weight

Sport, DK and worsted yarn can all make amigurumi. DK is a comfortable middle ground: small enough for detail but thick enough to work quickly. Always match your hook to the fabric you want.

Step 3: Use a smaller hook

Amigurumi normally needs a tighter fabric than scarves. Try a hook one or two sizes smaller than the yarn label suggests. Your stitches should close the gaps without becoming painful to work.

Step 4: Test a small sphere

Before making a large animal, crochet a tiny ball. Stuff it firmly and check whether the stitches hold their shape. If stuffing shows through, reduce the hook size or tighten your tension.

Color and care

Solid light colors make stitch counting easiest. For gifts, check the yarn's washing instructions so the finished toy can be cared for safely.
